# MVP Engineering · Job vocabulary

36 published word sets · eight words per job · 288 job-word entries.

Each set matches its job lesson and phase. Repeated words use a consistent definition and a new project example. Vocabulary supports the practical work; recognizing a definition does not establish measurement or fabrication competence.

## Assign a job

1. Open [Assign vocabulary](https://www.antocciphysics.com/admin/vocabulary/assign?class=33c5a178-998f-4158-aea9-f8589ade37b0).
2. Select the matching **MVP Job NN** word set.
3. Select the **trades-reset-N** Knowledge target whose statement is shown for that phase below.
4. Choose practice or quiz, students, due date, and requirements, then press **Assign vocabulary** or **Assign quiz**.

The six phase targets intentionally have no bulk default-word list: selecting a target preserves the eight words you chose for a job. The teacher can rate successive job assignments against the same phase Knowledge target. The installer creates no assignments or grades.

## Use during the job

- Before work: preview three or four unfamiliar words; show the real object or sketch.
- During work: students label the words on their drawing or use them while explaining a calculation.
- At handover: each student uses two words to explain a measurement, decision, or test result.
- Assign all eight for a short review, or uncheck words to give a smaller starting set.

Recognition practice is a reasonable starting setting; spelling is a separate demand. Keep the main lesson focused on measuring, making, checking, and explaining.
