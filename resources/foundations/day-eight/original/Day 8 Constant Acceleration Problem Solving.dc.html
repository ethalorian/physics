Worked braking distance

A car moves at +20 m/s and brakes at constant −4 m/s² until it stops. How far does it travel?

Solve alone · compare as a group · then check.
Your turnWork it out on paper. Then press Show answer to check.
Answer
0² = 20² + 2(−4)Δx
Δx = −400 / (−8) = +50 m

Time is unnecessary. As a check, t = 5 s and average velocity = 10 m/s, giving 50 m.

Vertical motion uses the same tools

A ball launches upward at 14 m/s. Ignore air resistance and use upward as positive, a = −9.8 m/s². Find the rise to its highest point.

Solve alone · compare as a group · then check.
Your turnWork it out on paper. Then press Show answer to check.
Answer

At the highest point v = 0.

0 = 14² + 2(−9.8)Δy, so Δy = 10 m. Acceleration is still downward at the top.

Before class
Skill day. Pacing is intentional.
Do the first problem fully on the board, then let pairs work. Pair work reduces math anxiety.
Strong math: push WHY an equation applies. Weaker math: give the equations and let them practice.
Most common error: wrong equation because given/asked wasn't listed first. Build the habit.
Reversal check: v₀ = +2 m/s, a = −2 m/s², t = 2 s gives Δx = 0 m but distance 2 m.
