Teacher only · notes & materials
Before class
Vocabulary with a reason.
The vector vs. scalar distinction is one of the deepest in physics — students who get it now save themselves grief in every later unit.
Common confusion: "displacement" as a synonym for "distance." Use the walk to make the difference physical.
The asteroid connection at the end is critical: the vocabulary is not arbitrary.
Materials
Whiteboard / chart paper
Open space for walking (gym, hallway, or rearranged room)
N/S/E/W signs posted around the room
Practice handout (4–5 distance vs. displacement problems)
Exit ticket slips
Reading
CPA: Hewitt Ch 4 §4.2 · Honors: Ch 4 §4.2–4.3
