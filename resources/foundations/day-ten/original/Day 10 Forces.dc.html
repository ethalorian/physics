Mass and weight are different

Mass: amount of matter/inertia, in kg.

Weight: gravitational force, in N.

Near Earth: use g ≈ 10 N/kg
Classroom approximation: weight = mass × 10.

Mass and weight
Fg = mg

Mass measures inertia in kilograms. Weight is a gravitational force in newtons.

Near Earth’s surface, use g = 9.8 m/s² unless a problem supplies another value.

A 5 kg object has weight ≈ 49 N

The foundation for FBDs.
Students must identify forces before they draw free body diagrams (Days 15–16).
Common confusion: "velocity is a force" or "inertia is a force." Inertia is resistance to force; velocity results from past forces.
