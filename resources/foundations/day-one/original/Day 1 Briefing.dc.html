Before class
Tone matters today.
Dramatic, not panicked: "We have a year to figure this out." Some students engage at once; some are skeptical. Both are fine.
Save the chart paper. In May, point back to a Day 1 question: "Now you can answer that."
Repeat "this is fiction; the physics is real" several times today and all week. This is a serious intellectual project.
