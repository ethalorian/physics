Average velocity and average speed
v̄ = Δx / Δt
Average speed = total distance / elapsed time

Average velocity carries direction. Average speed describes path length covered per second.

An instantaneous velocity describes a particular moment. An average summarizes an interval.

Before class
Beloved — and diagnostic.
Students who can match graphs can read them; students who can't, can't.
Watch for "moving fast" vs. "moving forward": a steep negative slope is fast in the negative direction.
By the end, students should see that all of NASA's trajectory data is position-vs-time data at huge scale.
