Reference & assumptions

The model we can use

Acceleration = velocity change ÷ time.

Example: 2 to 8 m/s in 3 s gives (8-2)÷3=2 m/s2.

Acceleration changes velocity

turning changes velocity even at steady speed.

Problem 2 · Think aloud

Worked example: braking on a bike

ThinkModelExplain

Worked example on your worksheet

A cyclist brakes while riding east.

Her speed drops steadily from 8 m/s to 2 m/s in 3 s.

Take east as positive.

The velocity change is 2 - 8 = -6 m/s.

Acceleration = -6 ÷ 3 = -2 m/s2.

She is still moving east, but her acceleration points west.

Pause & think

Follow the given example. Explain why each step is valid.
