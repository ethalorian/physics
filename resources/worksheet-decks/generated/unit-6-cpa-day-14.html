The model and assumptions on your sheetCPA · Unit 6 · Day 14

Worksheet reference

A converging lens brings paraxial parallel rays toward a focal point. A diverging lens spreads them as if from a focal point. For quantitative thin-lens cases: 1/f=1/do+1/di.
