Reference & assumptions

The model we can use

For meshed gears, output rpm=input rpm×driver teeth/driven teeth.

Wheel circumference C=πd.

Ideal speed=C×wheel rpm/60.

A larger driven gear reduces speed and increases ideal torque

losses and motor loading matter.
