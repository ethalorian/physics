Reference and limits

Useful ideas and equations

In this simplified calculation, Δv ≈ projectile momentum ÷ target mass when the target mass is much larger.

Sideways shift ≈ Δv × lead time for a straight-line comparison.

NASA’s DART demonstrated kinetic deflection.

The material ejected during the impact also transferred momentum.
