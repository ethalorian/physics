The Braking Car

A car at 30 m/s brakes to a stop in 90 m. What was its acceleration?

GIVEN  v₀ = 30 m/s · v = 0 · Δx = 90 m ASKED  a = ? NO TIME  → v² = v₀² + 2a(x − x₀)
0 = (30)² + 2a(90) a = −900 / 180 a = −5 m/s² negative — it's slowing. The sign is the physics.
