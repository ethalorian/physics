THE RIDDLE

If the Cart Pulls Back Equally,
How Does It Ever Move?

The horse-cart pair is internal — equal, opposite, and on different objects, so they cannot cancel. What moves the system is external: the ground pushing on the horse's feet.

ASTEROID THREAD

The Impactor Pushes —
the Asteroid Pushes Back

Equal forces, wildly different outcomes: the asteroid's velocity barely changes (huge mass); the spacecraft stops dead. You can deflect a massive object with a small one — if you can transfer the momentum. That word is Unit 3's.
