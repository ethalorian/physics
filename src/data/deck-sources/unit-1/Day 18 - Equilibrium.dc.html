2026-XJ Is Almost in Equilibrium

Solar gravity always pulls — so it's never truly balanced. But over a short leg the pull barely changes anything: approximately equilibrium. That's the formal justification for Day 5's constant-velocity model — and exactly where Unit 2 takes over.
