THE DERIVATION CPA SKIPS

Two Graphs, One Law — Why?

You have two SEPARATE one-variable claims. Combining them into a ∝ F/m is the SIMPLEST law consistent with both. That is a choice — an independence assumption — not a proof.

a ∝ 1/m + a ∝ F → F = ma
→ PACKET · DAY 12 — DERIVE F = MA U1 · 06/12
THE HUMAN CHOICE

You Just Defined the Newton

a ∝ F/m becomes a = F/m once we choose the proportionality constant to be 1. That single choice is what DEFINES the newton:

1 N gives 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s²
