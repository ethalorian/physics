NEWTON'S FIRST LAW

An object keeps its velocity — speed AND direction — unless a net force acts on it.

AT REST

Stays at rest. The tablecloth slides out; the cups stay put.

IN MOTION

Keeps moving. The car stops; you keep going — that's what seatbelts are for.
