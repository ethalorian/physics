TWO KINDS

Starting Is Harder Than Keeping Going

STATIC — BEFORE IT MOVES
f_s ≤ μ_s · N

Adjusts to match your push — up to a maximum. Then it lets go.

KINETIC — WHILE IT SLIDES
f_k = μ_k · N

Steady, and usually smaller: μ_s > μ_k.

Both depend on the normal force and the surface pair — not on speed, not on surface area.
