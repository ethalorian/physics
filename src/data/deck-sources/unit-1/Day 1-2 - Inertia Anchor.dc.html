Why? Build the Explanation Together

The jerk loads the bottom string to breaking FASTER than the mass's inertia will let it accelerate. The force never reaches the top. The mass resists the CHANGE in its motion.

Inertia is resistance to a change in motion — not just "amount of stuff."
