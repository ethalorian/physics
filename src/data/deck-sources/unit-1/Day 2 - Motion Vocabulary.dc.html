VOCABULARY

The Language of Motion

Position

Where an object is — a point, relative to an origin.

Sp. posición · Pt. posição

Distance

Total path length traveled. Scalar — no direction.

Sp. distancia · Pt. distância

Displacement

Change in position. Vector — has direction.

Sp. desplazamiento · Pt. deslocamento

Try it with your body: walk 5 steps north, 3 east, 5 south. Measure your DISTANCE walked vs. your DISPLACEMENT from start. Who's surprised how much they differ?
