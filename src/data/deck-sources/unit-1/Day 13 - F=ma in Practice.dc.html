THE ONE RULE

F Means NET Force

Net force is the vector sum of every force on the object — direction and sign included. The applied force alone is not enough.

1 · LIST FORCES → 2 · FIND NET → 3 · F = ma
WORKED EXAMPLE 20 N applied − 5 N friction = 15 N net · m = 5 kg a = 3 m/s²
