ONE DIMENSION FIRST

Plus Beats Minus

Pick a positive direction. Net force = everything pointing + minus everything pointing −. Then a = F_net / m. The sign convention is the bookkeeping.

WORKED EXAMPLE +30 N applied − 10 N friction = 20 N net · m = 5 kg a = 4 m/s²
