THE RULES

Six Rules, Every Diagram

01Just the object — reduce it to a dot.
02One arrow per force ON the object.
03Arrows point in the force's direction.
04Length proportional to magnitude.
05Label each: F_g · F_N · F_f · F_T · F_app
06ONLY forces on the object — nothing it exerts on other things.

Four Broken Diagrams
