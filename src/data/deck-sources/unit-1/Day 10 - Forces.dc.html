VOCABULARY · MEASURED IN NEWTONS

Five Forces You'll Name All Year

Gravity

F_g — pulls toward the mass. Always there.

Normal

F_N — the surface pushes back, perpendicular.

Friction

F_f — resists sliding, along the surface.

Tension

F_T — a rope or string pulls along itself.

Applied

F_app — the push WE control.

Scale check: 1 N ≈ the weight of a small apple. Forces are vectors — every arrow has a direction.

THE HONORS MOVE — DEFEND THE EXCLUSIONS

What Is NOT a Force

Momentum

A property of moving mass — not a push on it.

Inertia

RESISTS force — it isn't one.

Velocity

A RESULT of past forces — not a current push.
