**STOP 2.** 2.0 × 10²⁰ N vs a 700 N weight: about 3 × 10¹⁷ times bigger.

**STOP 4 · Pattern.** Biggest F: Earth–Sun. Surprise: the Sun pulls on the Moon (4.3 × 10²⁰ N) about twice as hard as Earth does (2.0 × 10²⁰ N). "Which changes most when r doubles?" Trick question: *every* pair drops to ¼, the same factor. In newtons, the biggest force loses the most.

**STOP 5 · Exit ticket.** r drops by a factor of 5, so F grows by 5² = **25×** (check: 9.5 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.8 × 10⁷ = 25).

## Formative checks
- Centre-to-centre distance, metres (not km), and the square on the **whole** distance (5 × 3.84 × 10⁸, then square).
- Ask for a predicted direction (bigger/smaller) before accepting a calculator answer.
