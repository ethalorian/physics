## Notes
- **STOP 1:** hand out the one-page NASA Day 1 Briefing before continuing. 5 minutes silent.
- **STOP 2:** groups of 3–4; 3 notices, 5+ wonders on chart paper; gallery walk.
- **STOP 3:** capture the class's questions. When you continue, sample questions sort into the six buckets (How fast? Where? When? How big? What's it made of? Can we stop it?). Compare with your class's list.
- **Unit titles:** your plan didn't list the seven unit titles, so the film uses placeholder titles built from the question categories. **Edit them in Tweaks → Unit titles** to match your course; the Unit 1 title also feeds the anchor-question screen.
- **STOP 4 · Exit ticket:** one question they really want answered, and whether physics can answer it or people must decide.

## Formative checks
- Tonight: sort exit-ticket questions into physics vs. social/political. Both are valid.
- Note students who didn't engage in notice/wonder; it could indicate concern about the premise. Check in privately tomorrow.
- Misconceptions to look for: thinking the asteroid is already visible, or that we already know exactly what it is.
