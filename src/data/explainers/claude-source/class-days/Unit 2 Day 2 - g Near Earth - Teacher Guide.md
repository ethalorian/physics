## Answers and what to listen for

**STOP 1 · Discuss.** Bowling ball: **bigger gravitational force** (F = mg, more mass). **Same acceleration** (a = F/m = g). This is today's key misconception check: equal acceleration does *not* mean equal force.

**STOP 2 · Your turn.** Run the live motion-detector drop first if you can. On screen: (0.10 s, 0.98 m/s) and (0.50 s, 4.90 m/s) → slope = 3.92 ÷ 0.40 ≈ **9.8 m/s²**. Check units: (m/s)/s = m/s². Both balls give the same slope. (With the detector above the ball, "away" is positive, so the velocity is positive.)

**Worked examples (on screen, 18–32 min).** 10 m: t = √(2 × 10 / 9.81) ≈ 1.43 s, v ≈ 14.0 m/s. 100 m: t ≈ 4.52 s, v ≈ 44.3 m/s. Ten times the height is only √10 ≈ 3.2 times the time.

**STOP 3 · Pairs (32–45 min).** Your packet problems. Predict first, then check with a stopwatch drop from a safe height (a stopwatch will run a little long because of reaction time).

**2026-XJ (no stop).** Near Earth's surface the asteroid would fall with the same g as a coin. Farther out the field weakens with distance from Earth's centre (2.8 × 10⁻⁸ m/s² at 1.2 × 10⁸ km, from Unit 1 Day 7). That distance dependence comes later this unit.

**STOP 4 · Exit ticket.** t = √(2 × 1 / 9.81) ≈ **0.45 s**.
