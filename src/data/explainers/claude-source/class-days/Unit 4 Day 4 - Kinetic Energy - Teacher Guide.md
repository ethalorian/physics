## Answers and what to listen for

**STOP 1 · Predict.** Students revisit Day 1's prediction. Energy **quadruples** (×4). Note: KE ×4 isn't automatically damage ×4 (Day 1's point).

**STOP 2 · Car.** ½(1500)(30²) = **675,000 J**; at 60 m/s: ½(1500)(60²) = **2,700,000 J**, exactly ×4.

**STOP 3 · The table.**
| Object | m (kg) | v (m/s) | p (kg·m/s) | KE (J) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bullet | 0.05 | 400 | 20 | 4,000 |
| sprinter | 70 | 10 | 700 | 3,500 |
| car | 1500 | 30 | 45,000 | 6.75 × 10⁵ |
| cruise ship | 5 × 10⁶ | 8 | 4 × 10⁷ | 1.6 × 10⁸ |
| 2026-XJ | 3.5 × 10¹¹ | 8,500 | 3.0 × 10¹⁵ | 1.3 × 10¹⁹ |

**The ranking that changed:** the sprinter has 35× the bullet's momentum, but the **bullet has more KE** (4,000 J vs 3,500 J) because speed counts twice in KE. 2026-XJ's 1.3 × 10¹⁹ J is the damage number cashed out on Day 19.

**STOP 4 · Work–energy theorem.** W_net = (10 N)(5 m) = 50 J = K_f − 0 → ½(1)v² = 50 → v² = 100 → **v = 10 m/s**.

**STOP 5 · Exit ticket.** K_f = 9 + 27 = 36 J → ½(2)v² = 36 → v² = 36 → **v = 6 m/s**. (Speed doubled, KE quadrupled.) The common error: setting ½mv² = 27 J, i.e. treating the work as the final KE (v ≈ 5.2 m/s).

**STOP 6 · Marzano on K2, K4, S2.**

## Formative checks
- Speed squared in every KE calculation.
- Net work equated with the **change** in KE; final KE kept distinct from ΔK.
