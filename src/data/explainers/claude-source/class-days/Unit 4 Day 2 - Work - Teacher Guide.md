## Answers and what to listen for

**STOP 1 · Your turn.** Box: W = (100 N)(5 m) = **500 J**. Bag: W = (50 N)(2 m) = **100 J**. Units: N·m = J.

**STOP 2 · Predict.** **Zero** work on the wall: it doesn't move, so d = 0. Students often say "lots, because I'm tired": the muscles do use chemical energy (it becomes heat in your body), but none is transferred to the wall.

**Book carry (on screen).** Your force on the book is up; the motion is sideways; there's no motion in the force's direction, so W = 0 (at steady speed; small horizontal pushes to start and stop are ignored). Tomorrow: forces at an angle.

**STOP 3 · Group practice (answers).**
1. 30 N push, cart moves 4 m: **120 J**
2. Hold a 40 N box still for 10 s: **0 J** (no displacement)
3. Crane lifts a 2,000 N beam 12 m: **24,000 J**
4. Waiter carries a tray level 8 m: **0 J** by the upward force on the tray
5. 15 N friction as a sled slides 6 m: **−90 J** (friction opposes the motion; preview of negative work; accept "90 J taken away")
6. 500 N on a car that doesn't budge: **0 J**

**STOP 4 · Exit ticket.** Lift: W = (20 N)(1.5 m) = **30 J**. Walk 3 m at the same height: **0 J more** by your upward force on the book (force vertical, motion horizontal).

**STOP 5 · Marzano on K1 and S1.** Asteroid link: gravity does work on 2026-XJ as it falls in, building its KE.

## Formative checks
- Displacement, not effort or time, appears in the work calculation.
- In the parallel-force case, students report joules and name the force acting on the chosen object.
