P4 · 06 · Add the Door Signal to Room Two

Replace room 2’s manual switch with the clothespin door detector; room 1 remains independently switched.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

A useful control responds to a condition. We will make room 2’s light act as a door-open signal without changing room 1.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. Two-room model, retained clothespin/foil switch, one 3 × 3-in card door, 1 × 3-in tongue, guide strip and 18 in tape. No added electrical load is required.

Finished means

  1. A two-room model with room 1 light switch and room 2 door-open signal.
  2. Revised schematic, layout and four-state table.

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Mark a 2 1/2 × 2 1/2-in door opening in room 2’s front wall, right jamb 1/2 in from the right outside corner; mark the left jamb 2 1/2 in farther toward the center divider.
  2. Measure tongue travel and locate the clothespin INSIDE room 2 so a shut door inserts a tongue pointing into the room; keep clips accessible from the open top.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Power OFF and remove room 2’s two switch leads from S2.
  2. Cut the door opening out of room 2’s front wall; preserve the surrounding wall. Tape a 3 × 3-in door over it along the left jamb to form an outward-opening hinge.
  3. Fold the 1 × 3-in tongue 1 in from a short end. Tape the 1-in leg vertically INSIDE the lower free edge of the door, fold at base level, so its 2-in leg points into room 2. Put clothespin inside the room with jaws facing the front wall and handle toward the rear. Shut door inserts tongue; outward opening withdraws it.
  4. Attach a loose guide loop just before the jaws, using the earlier detector method.
  5. Connect supply + to jaw A and jaw B to L2-A; keep L2-B return to −. Remove unused S2 from the model.
  6. Teacher checks circuit. Power ON and test four states: S1 off/door shut, S1 on/shut, S1 off/open, S1 on/open.
  7. Repeat each state three times. Reset the door by opening clothespin to reinsert tongue. Power OFF for storage.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. L1 follows only S1; L2 follows door position (shut OFF, open ON). All twelve state checks recorded.
  2. Door opens without snagging wires; reset method is printed on the model.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
