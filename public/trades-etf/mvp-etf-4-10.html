  1. Individually remeasure one component location or lead route using its original datum.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Write a two-minute sequence: 20 s purpose; 40 s four states; 30 s drawing/measurement; 30 s repair and evidence.
  2. Choose one real calculation from your notebook with given values, units and result; prepare to explain it without a teammate.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. The same task takes 30 s before a revision and 24 s after. Find the time reduction as a percentage of the original time.
  2. A task improves from 30 s to 24 s: time reduction = 6/30 × 100 = 20%.
  3. Choose a metric meaningful to your product and preserve the original procedure.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Demonstrate the exhibit and use actual evidence to support one improvement claim.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. A new partner operates the model using only its card. Mark any unclear instruction or snag.
  2. Power OFF before repair. Fix that one physical issue or rewrite the exact unclear instruction; if none was found, improve a missing terminal label.
  3. Repeat all four states after the change.
  4. Put model, schematic, layout and one test record on the table. A slideshow is not required.
  5. Rehearse the two-minute sequence. Each student operates and explains one distinct skill; trio roles rotate.
  6. Label setup/reset/power-off steps on the operator card. Store the checked exhibit with holder OFF.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. New operator can reset door and demonstrate independent branches from the card.
  2. Each student can show their own measured value and calculation; a decorative poster does not replace either.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
