MVP Engineering · Build, Measure, Improve

Four ETF projects. Trades math, accurate measuring, and drawing/plan interpretation in every project. 30 project meetings, four practice/revision meetings, and two exhibition meetings; about 110 minutes each. This sequence is not a fixed school calendar.

Read the plan → calculate → measure and lay out → build → inspect → revise. Use paper plans and your pocket notebook at the bench. Save one concise individual response in the app.

First meeting: printable stand build · Labeled assembly and test drawings

Prepare the working equipment

Each lesson specifies one required job, a daily kit, numbered directions and a defined test. Start with the card stand using folders and ordinary supplies. Plan thin card for trays/room models, matching 20-mL syringe/tube kits for delivery sleds, then 3-V lamps, two-AA holders, clothespins and supervised meter stations for controls. Teacher plans include demonstrations, inspection points and troubleshooting.
