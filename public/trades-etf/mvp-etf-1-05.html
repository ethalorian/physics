P1 · 05 · Inspect and Hand Over the Finished Tray

Deliver the three-bay tray, a matching top/front drawing and a completed fit inspection.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

A customer needs a working product and a drawing that matches it. An inspector must separate dimensional tolerance from functional fit.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. Both tray versions, original test supplies, final cut list and one blank inspection page.

Finished means

  1. A handed-over working tray, two-view drawing and honest inspection record.
  2. Each student completes the app transfer answer and a live tape/plan check.

Brief · 10 minutes · Today’s exact job

  1. Why this job: A customer needs a working product and a drawing that matches it. An inspector must separate dimensional tolerance from functional fit.
  2. Required today: Deliver the three-bay tray, a matching top/front drawing and a completed fit inspection.
  3. Look at the supplied labeled drawing. Find the finished object or test setup before starting.
  4. Read the kit list. Take the listed continuing project out of storage; name missing items before beginning.

Notebook: Title/date; one sentence naming today’s exact object and its purpose.

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Individually measure outside width, depth and one clear bay. Record intended | actual | difference | pass/rework.
  2. Use outside width 8 in ±1/8 and depth 6 in ±1/8 as classroom checks. Clear-bay fit is checked with its assigned object.

Notebook: Feature | required value | actual value | units | datum/endpoints. Keep repeat readings.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Draw the final top and front views with units, datum, actual partition positions and revision B.
  2. Write this three-line handover: what goes in each bay; how to move the tray (support the base); which joint was improved.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. A part should measure 8 in, with a stated tolerance of ±1/8 in. It measures 8 1/8 in. Calculate the signed error and decide whether it meets the dimensional tolerance.
  2. Actual 8 1/8 in − intended 8 in = +1/8 in.
  3. A teacher-set ±1/8-in tolerance includes both limits; whether this is suitable depends on the object and material.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Show your own tape reading, the matching plan dimension and the functional test record.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Exchange tray and drawings with another crew. Inspect without changing their work.
  2. Identify one mismatch between drawing and object; if none is found, record “no mismatch found” and the dimensions checked.
  3. Return the tray. Its owners correct the marked drawing or refit the inaccurate part, then repeat the affected measurement.
  4. Run the nine retrieval attempts with the original supplies.
  5. Give the receiving crew a 60-second handover using the object and drawing.
  6. Each student shows the teacher one tape reading and traces one part from plan to object. Store both versions in the labeled tray.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. All three supply types fit and can be retrieved without lifting the tray; note each failure.
  2. Drawing and product agree within the stated checks. A dimensional pass does not excuse a failed retrieval.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.

Explain · 10 minutes · Show the finished work

  1. Put these on the bench: A handed-over working tray, two-view drawing and honest inspection record.
  2. Each student completes the app transfer answer and a live tape/plan check.
  3. Complete the independent transfer problem below. These supplied numbers are practice values; they do not change the required build dimensions.
  4. A plan calls for 6 3/4 in ±1/8 in. State the acceptable interval. Does an actual 6 7/8-in part meet it? Give your own final measurement and notebook reference.
  5. Save one short individual answer with units, working and notebook page/date. Show the teacher your own physical measurement and plan interpretation. Store the labeled project and return shared supplies.

Notebook: Individual transfer calculation with units + page/date containing the practical evidence.
