P1 · 06 · Clinic: Make a Four-Center Mounting Template

Individually make a paper mounting template with four located centers; use the skill to inspect the tray.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

Installers locate holes from edges and centers. The paper template lets you practice that skill without drilling.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. One 8 1/2 × 5 1/4-in paper rectangle per student, one spare sheet and the completed tray.

Finished means

  1. Two individually marked paper templates and one tray position check.
  2. Notebook includes coordinates, spacing calculation and any correction.

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Choose lower-left as (0,0). Mark x = 3/4 and 7 3/4 in along bottom and top.
  2. Mark y = 3/4 and 4 1/2 in along left and right. Draw light crossing guide lines.

Notebook: Feature | required value | actual value | units | datum/endpoints. Keep repeat readings.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Copy a center table: left-bottom (3/4,3/4), right-bottom (7 3/4,3/4), left-top (3/4,4 1/2), right-top (7 3/4,4 1/2), all in inches.
  2. Calculate the horizontal and vertical center spacing before marking the centers.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. A mounting template is 8 1/2 in wide and 5 1/4 in high. Each center is 3/4 in from its nearest edge. Find the horizontal and vertical center spacings.
  2. Horizontal: 8 1/2 − 2 × 3/4 = 7 in. Vertical: 5 1/4 − 2 × 3/4 = 3 3/4 in.
  3. Overall dimensions and center spacing are different quantities.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Show the datum, one pair of coordinates and the separate horizontal spacing checks for A and B.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Lay out the rectangle independently. Cut its outside edge only.
  2. Use the four guide-line intersections to draw four small crosses.
  3. Exchange templates; checker measures offsets from the named datum without correcting marks.
  4. Mark an inaccurate cross with a small circle, then make the corrected cross beside it. Keep the first attempt visible.
  5. Draw revision B with horizontal offsets of 1 in from each side; keep vertical offsets 3/4 in.
  6. Return to the tray and independently check one partition’s position from its left outside edge. Correct its drawing if needed.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. All four original centers are within ±1/16 in of their specified positions.
  2. Revision B horizontal spacing is checked separately; retain both versions.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
