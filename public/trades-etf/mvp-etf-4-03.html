P4 · 03 · Measure Voltage Across the Source and Lamp

At the teacher meter station, measure DC voltage across source and lamp, then diagnose one opened lamp connection.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

Voltage is a difference between two points. Correct probe placement helps trace an open path without guessing or rewiring randomly.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. Working detector; supervised digital multimeter with black lead in COM and red in V input; the demonstrated DC-voltage range above 3 V (typically 20 V on a manual-range meter).

Finished means

  1. A restored working detector.
  2. Actual state-labeled source/lamp voltage readings and identified open connection.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. A source has a nominal rating of 3.0 V. A measurement gives 2.8 V. Calculate measured minus nominal voltage.
  2. A nominal 3.0-V source reads 2.8 V: difference = −0.2 V.
  3. A nominal rating is not a guaranteed measurement; interpret readings with the circuit condition and instrument resolution.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Point to the two measurement points and show signed readings with units.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Switch holder OFF while placing clips safely. Teacher checks that probes cannot bridge adjacent terminals.
  2. Turn ON. Hold insulated probe handles and take the demonstrated source reading, then lamp A-to-B reading with tongue out.
  3. Insert tongue and repeat lamp reading in OFF state. Do not infer every unseen circuit condition from this single reading.
  4. Power OFF. Teacher removes the lead from lamp terminal A and parks the loose clip away from other conductors.
  5. Power ON under supervision. Note lamp off; measure source then lamp using the same test points.
  6. Power OFF, identify the disconnected terminal by tracing J, reconnect it and repeat the working lamp reading.
  7. Return probes to station and store detector with holder OFF.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. Teacher directly observes each student’s correct DC-V mode and across-two-points connection.
  2. Working source remains available during the open-lamp fault; actual readings depend on meter resolution and contact. No readings are invented when a station turn is pending.
