Why this job?

Installers need both a layout and a schematic. One locates a component; the other tells what connects to it.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. One 8 1/2 × 5 1/4-in paper sheet per student, completed model and final schematic. No drilling.

Finished means

  1. An individual mounting template and corrected model documentation.
  2. Separate physical-location and circuit-path explanations.
