P4 · 01 · Build a Door-Open Light Signal

Make a card door that holds a clothespin switch open when shut; opening the door pulls out a card tongue and lights one 3-V lamp.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

A control turns a physical event into an electrical signal. The door must operate the switch; a student’s hidden finger is not the detector.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. 3-V two-AA switched holder, one 3-V lamp/holder, three alligator leads, one wooden spring clothespin, two 1 × 2-in foil strips, base 10 × 8 in, fixed wall 4 × 4 in, door 3 × 4 in, 1 × 3-in thin-card tongue and 24 in tape.

Finished means

  1. One working card-door/clothespin light detector with labeled terminals.
  2. Schematic, route lengths and five OFF/ON test rows.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Tape the 4 × 4-in fixed wall upright along y=3 in, from x=0 to x=4; brace it with two small folded scrap triangles cut from a 2 × 2-in square.
  2. Tape the 3 × 4-in door to the wall’s right vertical edge. Shut, it runs from x=4 to x=7 along y=3. It opens toward the FRONT of the base (smaller y).
  3. Wrap one foil strip over the inside face of each clothespin jaw and around the outside for a clip contact. Tape foil backs securely; keep the two foil strips separate except at the jaw contact.
  4. Glue is not required: put clothespin BEHIND the shut door, with jaws near (6 1/2,4 1/2) and handle extending toward the rear edge. Jaws face toward the door. Tape the lower handle to the base, keeping spring/upper handle free.
  5. Fold the tongue 90 degrees at its 1-in mark. Tape the 1-in leg vertically to the BACK of the door near its free edge, center x=6 1/2, with the fold at base level. The 2-in leg projects BEHIND the shut door toward increasing y and enters the jaws. Opening the door toward the front withdraws it. Adjust jaw position slightly for an easy sliding fit.
  6. With holder switched OFF, clip the three leads exactly as J1’s table states. Keep exposed clips apart except at intended terminals.
  7. Teacher checks the loop. Turn holder ON. Tongue inserted should separate foil and turn lamp off; swinging door open should withdraw tongue so jaws close and lamp turns on.
  8. Reset by gently opening the clothespin and reinserting tongue while closing the door. The opening action must trigger automatically; manual reset is allowed and stated.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. Open and reset the door five times. Record lamp OFF when shut and ON when opened in every trial.
  2. Power OFF after the test. If battery or wire feels warm, switch off immediately and have teacher inspect the wiring.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
