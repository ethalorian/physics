P4 · 05 · Wire Two Independently Lit Rooms

Build a two-room card model on an 8 × 6-in base; wire each 3-V lamp through its own switch in parallel.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

Independent controls require independent branches sharing a source. Testing all four switch states proves more than simply lighting both lamps.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. 3-V holder, two 3-V lamps/holders, two SPST switches, eight alligator leads, base 8 × 6 in, two outer walls 8 × 3 in, three wall blanks 6 × 3 in and 36 in tape. Retain door detector separately for next lesson.

Finished means

  1. Two-room model with independently controlled lamps and accessible wiring.
  2. Matched schematic/layout and twelve state-test results.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. Two independent switches each have ON and OFF states. List all combinations and the expected lamp outputs for two independent branches.
  2. Two independent on/off switches give 2 × 2 = 4 states: off/off, on/off, off/on, on/on.
  3. Testing only both-on does not establish independent control.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Cut base and wall blanks. Tape 8 × 3-in front/rear walls upright on the base.
  2. Dry-fit and trim left/right/divider 6 × 3-in blanks to the gap between front/rear walls. Tape outside walls at base edges and divider centered on x=4.
  3. Tape lamp holders and switches at their marked positions. Keep terminals accessible; route leads over the open wall tops.
  4. Holder OFF: connect + to S1-1, S1-2 to L1-A, L1-B to −. These are the first branch’s three leads.
  5. Connect + to S2-1, S2-2 to L2-A, L2-B to − with three more leads. Share the marked supply terminals without letting + clips touch − clips.
  6. Teacher checks branches and combined lamp rating. Turn holder ON and test all four states in order.
  7. Correct any misconnection with holder OFF; repeat all four states. Label rooms 1/2 and lamps L1/L2.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. 00: neither lamp; 10: L1 only; 01: L2 only; 11: both. Repeat the four-state sequence three times.
  2. Changing one switch must not change the other lamp. Record all twelve checks.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
