P2 · 04 · Build the Scale Room and Working Door

Make the 8 × 6-in community-room shell with 4-in walls, a hinged south door and movable table tokens.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

A coordinated plan and elevation must describe the same opening. You will transfer real dimensions to a physical model.

Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. Thin folder/card stock for the five room parts, previous floor layout/tokens, 48 in tape. Base 8 × 6 in; N/S walls 8 × 4 in; E/W blanks 6 × 4 in (trim to fit between N/S); roof 8 × 6 in. South door: left jamb 1 in from west end, opening 1 1/2 in wide × 3 1/2 in high. Roof is made on day 6.

Finished means

  1. A four-wall room model with working hinged south door and two tables; no roof yet.
  2. Coordinated top/front drawings and at least four model scale conversions.

Brief · 10 minutes · Today’s exact job

  1. Why this job: A coordinated plan and elevation must describe the same opening. You will transfer real dimensions to a physical model.
  2. Required today: Make the 8 × 6-in community-room shell with 4-in walls, a hinged south door and movable table tokens.
  3. Look at the supplied labeled drawing. Find the finished object or test setup before starting.
  4. Read the kit list. Take the listed continuing project out of storage; name missing items before beginning.

Notebook: Title/date; one sentence naming today’s exact object and its purpose.

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Mark base 8 × 6 in and N/S walls 8 × 4 in. Mark E/W blanks 6 × 4 in.
  2. On S mark door jambs at x=1 and 2 1/2 in, and lintel 3 1/2 in above the bottom edge.

Notebook: Feature | required value | actual value | units | datum/endpoints. Keep repeat readings.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Draw south elevation 8 × 4 in with the same door dimensions. Label all model dimensions in inches and write the full-size scale.
  2. Copy the five-part cut list and label outside faces N/S/E/W.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. Use a model scale of 1/2 in = 1 ft. Find the model dimensions for a 16 × 12-ft room and a 3-ft-wide door.
  2. 16 ft × 1/2 in/ft = 8 in; 12 ft × 1/2 in/ft = 6 in; 3-ft door = 1 1/2 in.
  3. Both plan and elevation must use the same chosen model scale.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Show the door at 90°, its matching plan/elevation location, and four model conversions.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Cut base and four walls. On S cut the right jamb and top of the door; fold along the left jamb to leave a hinged door flap. The bottom edge is already free.
  2. Tape N and S upright on top of the base with outer faces flush to the base edges; keep the door free of tape.
  3. Dry-fit E/W BETWEEN N/S. Mark the actual inside gap and trim their width so the shell closes without bowing.
  4. Tape E/W to base and adjoining walls. Use small inside and outside tape pieces at each joint.
  5. Label north and datum SW on the base. Return the two table tokens to their recorded positions.
  6. Swing the door inward at least 90 degrees. Move the person token from entrance to each table.
  7. Adjust only table positions if they block the door/route; update their positions on the plan.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. Base is 8 × 6 in and wall height 4 in, each within ±1/8 in. Door opening dimensions and offset are within ±1/16 in.
  2. Door opens 90 degrees without touching furniture; top and front drawings agree with actual door location.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.

Notebook: Individual transfer calculation with units + page/date containing the practical evidence.
