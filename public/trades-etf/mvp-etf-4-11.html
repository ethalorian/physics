Daily kit · one crew

Each student: tape measure, pencil and graph notebook. Per crew: ruler, scissors and masking tape. Prepared control exhibit, operator card, notebook and final drawings; teacher roster for four separate skill checks.

Finished means

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Each student measures one assigned component position or lead route without partner help, naming endpoints and units.
  2. Compare measured value with the student’s final drawing and record signed difference.

Notebook: Feature | required value | actual value | units | datum/endpoints. Keep repeat readings.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Mark where your notebook shows an actual repair, retest and calculation.
  2. Read the supplied tolerance transfer problem independently; state both limits before deciding acceptance.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. A part should measure 5 1/2 in ±1/8 in. State the acceptable interval. Inspect an actual dimension of 5 3/4 in and calculate how far it is outside the interval.
  2. Target 5 1/2 in ±1/8 in gives 5 3/8 through 5 5/8 in. An actual 5 3/4 in is 1/8 in above the upper limit.
  3. Tolerance is a stated acceptance range; function still needs testing.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Show your own measurement, tolerance decision and functional evidence.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Set up exhibit with power OFF; teacher inspects connections before operation.
  2. Demonstrate four control states to the visiting crew. Rotate operator, drawing explainer and inspector roles.
  3. Visitor records one specific observation: correct function, unclear step or located mismatch.
  4. Power OFF, correct that exact issue if present and repeat affected states; if none, complete the full final four-state check.
  5. At the teacher station each student: traces one branch; measures a physical dimension; solves the tolerance calculation; explains a named repair and retest.
  6. Record any still-missing skill as pending and complete a focused retry while other students inventory and store equipment.
  7. Power OFF, separate cells only under the kit storage routine, count parts and submit the single individual app response.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. All four control states demonstrated or the remaining fault precisely identified.
  2. Teacher records math, measuring, plan interpretation and build/revision separately for every student.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
