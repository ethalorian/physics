P2 · 06 · Close the Room and Count the Material

Make a removable roof and a materials takeoff for the model’s walls, door opening and roof.

Name: __________________ Date: __________ · 110 minutes

Why this job?

Estimators subtract openings and distinguish surface area from purchased sheet quantities. A removable roof lets the next worker inspect the interior.

Daily kit · one crew

Finished means

Measure · 15 minutes · Measure and mark

  1. Measure the actual outside roof-bearing rectangle and compare with 8 × 6 in.
  2. Measure door opening, not the flap including any overlap. Record the model’s actual dimensions separately from nominal takeoff values.

Plan · 15 minutes · Read the plan and work the math

  1. Calculate nominal wall area: two 8 × 4 plus two 6 × 4; subtract door area once. Add 8 × 6 roof separately.
  2. Draw the roof underside with two locator tabs near the north/south edges. These keep the roof from sliding; it stays lift-off.

Notebook: Use the named sketch/table in the directions. Label views, dimensions, units and revision.

Worked example · practice values

These supplied values teach the method. Build from today’s required plan dimensions; record your own actual test data.

  1. A wall is 12 × 8 ft and contains a 3 × 7-ft opening. Find the net covering area, then add a supplied planning allowance of 10%.
  2. A 12 × 8-ft wall minus a 3 × 7-ft opening: 96 − 21 = 75 ft². With a stated 10% allowance: 82.5 ft².
  3. Allowance is a planning choice, not a universal trade rule; purchase quantities depend on usable stock sizes.

Build along · one move at a time

Maker acts. Checker compares with the plan. Swap at the pause. Use the complete numbered directions below.

Stop & show

Remove and replace the roof. Show which area you subtracted for the door.

Make · 45 minutes · Numbered bench directions

  1. Cut roof 8 × 6 in. Mark underside and north edge.
  2. Fold each 2 × 1-in strip at its midpoint to make an L with 1-in legs.
  3. Place roof upside down. Tape one leg of a tab near the center of its north edge; locate the fold just inside the actual north wall position.
  4. Fit the roof. The free tab leg drops INSIDE the north wall. Mark and attach the second tab to fit inside the south wall.
  5. Lift and replace the roof three times. Trim tab width or adjust its location if it catches a wall.
  6. Write a small material label under the roof: nominal wall covering, roof area and actual card used.

Notebook: Label made parts and note any changed dimension. A short labeled sketch is enough; do not write an essay.

Test · 15 minutes · Test and inspect

  1. Roof covers the wall tops and lifts off without pulling walls apart; repeat three times.
  2. Takeoff subtracts the door once: wall covering 106 3/4 in²; roof 48 in². Treat overlaps and actual stock cutting waste separately.

Notebook: Copy the specified trial table; include each pass/fail or measured value and the change made.
