At the workbench

Equal on both sides.

01

Center a length

Measure between the two ends. Divide that length by 2, then measure that distance from an end and draw a line across the material.

15 ÷ 2 = 7 1/2 in

This locates the halfway line along its length.

02

Center a rectangular face

Halve the width and height. Mark half the width from the left and half the height down from the top. Their intersection is the center point.

10 × 6 in → X = 5, Y = 3 in

On a true rectangle, corner-to-corner diagonals also meet at its center.

03

Between two marks

Subtract the first position from the second. Halve the gap, then add the first position to locate the midpoint from the left end.

3 + (15 − 3) ÷ 2 = 9 in

Half of the gap is a distance from the first mark, not from the end of the stock.

Halving a fraction? Double its denominator, then simplify. Half of 3/8 is 3/16; half of 1/16 is 1/32. For a mixed number, halve the whole number and fraction and combine them.

Check your mark from both ends. For a face, also check from the top and bottom.
