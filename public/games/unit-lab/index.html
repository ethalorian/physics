Unit reference

1 m = 100 cm
1 km = 1000 m
1 h = 3600 s
1 min = 60 s
1 kg = 1000 g
1 N = 1 kg·m/s²

m: length · s: time · kg: mass
m/s: speed · m/s²: acceleration
kg/m³: density
