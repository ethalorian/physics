MAKE IT YOURS

Sound & timing

If taps on the beat keep reading LATE, increase the offset. If they read EARLY, decrease it. Wired headphones usually need less adjustment.

How to play

Use D, F, J, K for four-key levels, or S, D, F, J, K, L for Overdrive. Tap when notes meet the pads. Hold long notes to the tail and press chords together. Touch pads work too.

Perfect ±50 ms · Great ±100 ms · Good ±160 ms. Extra taps break your streak. Holds score once at the head and once at the tail.

Every song and level has its own ranked board. One credit plays your selected chart. Practice is always free.
