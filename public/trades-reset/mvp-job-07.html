Job 07 · Space five mounting centers

A paper mounting strip with equal centers and checked edge offsets.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Tapes, paper strips joined end-to-end, pencils, graph notebook.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Five objects make how many spaces between the first and last? Draw before calculating.
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Make or join a 30-in paper strip and mark a datum at its left end.
  2. Reserve a 3-in margin to the first and last center; measure the usable center span.
  3. Write the number of centers separately from the number of intervals.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate every center coordinate before making marks.
  2. Draw the strip with dimension arrows to centers, not to hypothetical object edges.
  3. Choose a classroom check tolerance of 1/8 in and write it on the work order.

Worked example

Five centers span a 30-in strip with 3-in end margins. Find spacing and all positions. Spacing = (overall − 2×margin)/(centers − 1). (30−6)/4 = 6 in. Positions from datum: 3, 9, 15, 21, 27 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Lay out all centers from the SAME datum; do not step an unverified interval down the strip.
  2. A partner checks end margins and every interval independently.
  3. Correct a mark with a new symbol and revision note so the final center is unambiguous.

Test · 20 min

  1. Five center marks are present and the four intervals agree with the calculation within 1/8 in.
  2. Both end margins are checked.
  3. Each student explains the denominator without using a memorized rule alone.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

Six centers go on a 42-in strip with 6-in end margins. Find spacing and the first three coordinates.
