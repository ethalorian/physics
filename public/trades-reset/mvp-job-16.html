Job 16 · Place the supports

A support layout with equal spans and a load-path explanation.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper beam/deck, book or folded-paper supports, tape, light identical loads.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A 24-in model has supports at both ends and three inside. How many spans are created?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Build a 24-in paper deck strip or use a scaled 12-in model with the scale labeled.
  2. Locate end supports and three internal support centerlines.
  3. Measure the actual support positions from a common datum.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw the centerline layout and calculate all support coordinates.
  2. Place a proposed load and draw arrows showing a possible path into supports.
  3. Predict which unsupported region will bend most; do not assume equal reactions for every load location.

Worked example

Place five support centerlines from 0 to 24 in at equal spacing. Span = end-to-end distance/(supports − 1). Four spans of 6 in; support centers 0, 6, 12, 18, 24 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build the layout and apply a small load at one span midpoint.
  2. Remove one internal support in the paper model and repeat using the same load.
  3. Restore or relocate a support to reduce the observed bend; record revised coordinates.

Test · 20 min

  1. The model matches the calculated centerline spacing.
  2. Observations distinguish span change from material change.
  3. Each student explains how a missing support changes the path and unsupported distance.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 36-in layout has supports at both ends and two inside at equal spacing. Give all coordinates and the span. How many spans remain if one internal support is removed?
