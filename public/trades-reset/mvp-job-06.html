Job 06 · Make a replacement template

A removable paper template for a desk mat, drawer liner, or label panel.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper, tape, scissors, notebook, tape measure; an approved reachable surface.

Brief · 12 min

  1. How could someone make a replacement piece later without access to the original surface?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Choose a small reachable rectangular feature. Name the part and datum; measure width, length, and any corner obstruction.
  2. Choose and state a 1/8-in edge gap for this paper template where appropriate. It is a design choice, not a building requirement.
  3. Have a partner repeat one critical measurement before cutting.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate your template dimensions and label a sketch NTS.
  2. Add any measured cutout; locate it from the datum rather than from a guessed center.
  3. Write a short fabrication note naming the source measurement and clearance.

Worked example

A 12 × 18 in recess needs a liner with a 1/8-in gap on every edge. Find liner size. Liner dimension = recess dimension − 2 × edge gap. 11 3/4 × 17 3/4 in. Clearance is subtracted when the part must fit inside an opening.

Make · 28 min

  1. Join paper if needed, mark the template, and cut it.
  2. Set it in place without adhesive. Mark interference points on the template.
  3. Revise the template and transfer final dimensions to a clean work order.

Test · 20 min

  1. Template fits without buckling and accounts for the selected edge gaps.
  2. A partner can reproduce one critical dimension from the work order.
  3. Show an independent subtraction and a documented test result.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

An opening is 10 1/2 in wide. A template needs 1/4 in clearance on each side. Find its width. Compare this subtraction with the additions used to design the tray.
