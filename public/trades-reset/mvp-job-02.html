Job 02 · Survey one school zone

A notebook survey another pair can use to locate three features.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Tapes, notebooks, pencils, loose paper; teacher-selected classroom or hallway zones.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Estimate a desk width, then measure it. Convert 3 ft 4 in to inches. Why must every survey name a starting point?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Choose a reachable rectangular zone and draw an NTS plan. Name one fixed corner A as the datum and mark two directions from it.
  2. Measure two boundaries and locate three features from A. Each student takes at least one measurement. Label whether a dimension goes to an edge or center.
  3. Independently remeasure one boundary. Record both readings, the difference, and the resolution of the tape. Keep people moving through the hallway.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Build one dimension string with at least three segments and compare its sum to the direct overall measurement.
  2. Write the discrepancy with units. Decide which endpoint or reading needs checking; do not force the numbers to agree.
  3. Prepare a clean drawing with datum, orientation, three features, endpoints, and units. Mark NTS unless you deliberately draw to scale.

Worked example

Along a wall, two segments measure 2 ft 8 in and 3 ft 7 in. Find their combined length in feet/inches and inches. Total inches = 12 × feet + inches; add in a common unit. 32 + 43 = 75 in = 6 ft 3 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Exchange drawings with another pair. Use only their drawing to locate the three features.
  2. Mark an unclear endpoint or missing dimension on a separate review slip. Return it to the author.
  3. Remeasure as needed and issue a revised drawing with date and revision letter.

Test · 20 min

  1. The receiving pair can identify all three features and the datum.
  2. Overall length and segment sum are both shown, with any discrepancy explained at the tape resolution.
  3. Each student shows one actual tape reading to the teacher.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A surveyed boundary is 8 ft 3 in. Two segments are 31 in and 42 in. Find the missing segment in inches and feet/inches. Name one change that made your survey easier to use.
