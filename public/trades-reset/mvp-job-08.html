Job 08 · Draw a plan to scale

A scale plan and cut-paper equipment pieces for a surveyed classroom zone.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Notebook, graph paper, rulers/tapes, scissors, pencils.

Brief · 12 min

  1. If 1 drawing inch represents 2 actual feet, is a 6-ft wall drawn as 3 in or 12 in?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Use the school-zone survey or remeasure a new approved zone.
  2. Record boundary sizes and two movable equipment footprints. Identify features that must remain clear.
  3. Choose a scale that fits the available sheet. Record actual dimensions even when drawing to scale.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw boundaries and datum at the selected scale.
  2. Calculate dimensions for two paper equipment pieces; label each with its real size.
  3. Propose a layout using existing observed circulation and teacher-defined model clearances; do not claim code approval.

Worked example

Draw a 12 × 8 ft zone at 1 in = 2 ft. A desk footprint is 4 × 2 ft. Drawing inches = actual feet / 2. Zone = 6 × 4 in; desk = 2 × 1 in. A 1 1/2-in drawing gap represents 3 actual feet.

Make · 28 min

  1. Cut and move the paper equipment pieces to test two arrangements.
  2. Measure the drawing gaps and convert them back to actual dimensions.
  3. Have a partner recover two real dimensions from your drawing without your help.

Test · 20 min

  1. Scale, datum, real dimensions, and feature labels are visible.
  2. Paper equipment uses the same scale as the room.
  3. The proposal respects the constraints supplied by the teacher and clearly marks any unverified condition.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

At 1 in = 3 ft, a drawing shows a 2 1/2-in wall and a 3/4-in gap. Find both actual lengths, including feet/inches for the gap.
