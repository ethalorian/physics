Job 20 · Coordinate a dry service bay

A plan and elevation showing two routes and a resolved crossing.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Graph paper, colored strips, tape, ruler; a 24 ×18 in tabletop paper bay.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Two routes can look separate in plan and still collide in elevation. What views does the installer need?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Draw a 24 ×18 in bay and datum. Place a horizontal service strip and a crossing strip with a stated model width.
  2. Set a model crossing envelope: usable depth 6 in, upper route thickness 1 in, lower route thickness 1 1/2 in, required model gap 1/2 in.
  3. Label these as supplied classroom constraints, not actual trade clearance rules.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw both plan and side view at the crossing.
  2. Specify the order of routes and locate their edges and centerlines from common references.
  3. List any assumed support thickness or bend allowance separately.

Worked example

How much depth do both routes and the model gap occupy? Required depth = upper thickness + gap + lower thickness. 1+1/2+1 1/2=3 in; 6−3=3 in remain for offsets or other stated allowances.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build colored paper strips and a folded side-view mockup of the crossing.
  2. Have another pair check all route envelopes and gaps.
  3. Revise a collision or an ambiguous dimension and rebuild the crossing.

Test · 20 min

  1. Plan and elevation describe the same crossing.
  2. The depth budget includes every stated thickness and gap.
  3. The model passes its classroom constraints and lists any unknown real-system requirements.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 5-in depth must hold a 1 1/4-in route, a 2-in route, and 3/4-in gap. Find spare depth. If one route grows by 1 1/2 in, quantify the conflict.
