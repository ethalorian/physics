Job 11 · Inspect a layout discrepancy

An inspection report with signed differences and a corrected paper layout.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper-marker layout from Lesson 3 or fresh 30 × 40 in model, tapes, notebook.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A target is 40 in and a reading is 40 3/8 in. What does a positive difference tell the person correcting it?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Lay out a 30 × 40 in rectangle with one deliberately misplaced corner, chosen by the teacher.
  2. Without seeing the planted error, measure all four sides, diagonals, and center coordinates.
  3. Record specified and measured values in separate columns.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate signed differences and absolute magnitudes for all measured constraints.
  2. Mark pass/check for each criterion using the teacher’s classroom tolerance.
  3. Propose the smallest correction that addresses the pattern of readings.

Worked example

Requested length 40 in; measured 40 3/8 in; assigned tolerance ±1/4 in. Decide acceptance. Signed difference = measured − requested. +3/8 in is outside ±1/4 in; the dimension is too long. A 39 7/8-in reading differs by −1/8 in and is inside this interval.

Make · 28 min

  1. Move a corner or center marker according to the inspection plan.
  2. Remeasure affected sides and both diagonals.
  3. Have the inspecting pair sign a report naming measurements, dates, and remaining limits.

Test · 20 min

  1. Each decision matches the stated tolerance, including the exact boundary.
  2. Both before and after readings are retained.
  3. The report distinguishes an actual reading from a predicted correction.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A center must be 18 in from datum with ±1/8 in tolerance. Evaluate 17 7/8 in and 18 3/16 in. Give the signed differences.
