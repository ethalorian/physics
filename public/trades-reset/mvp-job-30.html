Job 30 · Coordinate the system proposal

A paper model integrating an enclosure, low-voltage route, material estimate, and test plan.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper/folders, tape, ruler/tape, notebooks; optional approved light kit.

Equipment alternative: Build the enclosure and a labeled paper service route; provide a specific circuit test plan instead of claiming operation.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A layout, thermal layer, and wire route each work separately. Where might they conflict when combined?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Design a small paper display enclosure with a removable cover and optional approved low-voltage light.
  2. Measure the chosen base footprint and required equipment envelope.
  3. List accessibility, heat, routing, and material assumptions; keep paper away from any component the teacher identifies as warm.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw plan and side view with service path and removable access.
  2. Calculate paper area, a feasible piece layout, and a parts list.
  3. Choose acceptance tests for access, fit, and circuit operation if hardware is used.

Worked example

A paper enclosure has a 12 ×8 in base and 4-in side height. Find total area of base plus four walls, without a lid. Area = LW +2 LH +2 WH. 96+96+64=256 in² before tabs or cutting waste.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build the paper enclosure and route model; install only teacher-approved parts.
  2. Exchange models and have another pair follow the access/removal procedure.
  3. Revise one interference or unclear drawing dimension.

Test · 20 min

  1. Model, drawing, and estimate agree.
  2. Access is demonstrated without tearing the model.
  3. Electrical/thermal claims distinguish measured evidence from proposed tests.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 10 ×6 in base has 3-in walls and no lid. Find base-plus-wall area before tabs. Explain one coordination conflict you tested or would need to test.
