Job 14 · Test the effect of span

A graph and recommendation connecting span to paper-beam performance.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Identical folded paper beams, book supports, tape/ruler, light identical loads.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Would you predict the same beam to perform equally across 4, 6, and 8 in spans? What must remain unchanged?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Make matching beams from one fold pattern. Set up 4, 6, and 8 in support spans.
  2. Choose a fixed load count that does not immediately collapse the weakest setup, established by a small pilot trial.
  3. Measure midspan height before and after loading using the same datum; record the difference as deflection.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Predict the span–deflection trend and draw labeled axes: span in inches, deflection in inches.
  2. Plan three fresh-specimen readings per span or explicitly label repeated readings on one specimen.
  3. Keep beam shape, paper amount, load, placement, and supports the same.

Worked example

At fixed load, midspan height changes from 2 in to 1 3/4 in. Find deflection. Deflection = unloaded height − loaded height. 1/4 in downward. Record it as a magnitude and direction; do not confuse the remaining height with the change.

Make · 28 min

  1. Take readings at each span and graph your actual results.
  2. Repeat a surprising result after checking the datum and beam condition.
  3. Choose a span for a model shelf and justify it with evidence, not a universal structural rule.

Test · 20 min

  1. Graph axes carry quantities and units.
  2. A comparison separates controlled load from changing span.
  3. Recommendation acknowledges that paper-beam results do not rate a full-size shelf.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

An unloaded beam midpoint is 2 1/4 in above the desk and becomes 1 7/8 in under load. Calculate deflection. If load also changed between spans, why would the comparison be ambiguous?
