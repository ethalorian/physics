Job 05 · Handle a change order

A revised tray work order, new prototype, and measured comparison.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Original notebook/tray, paper, tape, scissors, pencils.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A customer changes one requirement. What should stay traceable between the original and revised drawing?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Read your original tray dimensions. Mark the original gap and wall height on a fresh sketch.
  2. New requirement: increase clearance from 1/8 to 1/4 in on EACH side; retain 3/4 in walls.
  3. Remeasure the notebook if the original endpoint record is unclear. Preserve the original design as revision A.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Individually calculate the change to both base and blank for your notebook.
  2. Make revision B with changed dimensions highlighted and unchanged wall height shown.
  3. Check whether the new blank fits available paper. State whether the paper orientation or joining plan must change.

Worked example

An original inside base is 5 3/4 × 3 3/4 in. Each side gap grows by 1/8 in. Find the new base and blank. Total dimension change = 2 × increase per-side gap. Base grows by 1/4 in to 6 × 4 in. With 3/4-in walls, blank = 7 1/2 × 5 1/2 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Trade the revised work order and build a second tray.
  2. Place original and revised trays side by side with the same notebook.
  3. Record whether the larger gaps satisfy the changed requirement and whether the walls still stand.

Test · 20 min

  1. Revision B changes both dimensions by the correct total gap increase.
  2. The builder can identify the current revision and all critical dimensions.
  3. A physical comparison supports the change claim.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A different customer keeps the base unchanged but increases EACH wall from 3/4 to 1 in. How much does each blank dimension grow? Explain why the inside base does not have to change.
