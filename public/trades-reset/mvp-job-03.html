Job 03 · Lay out a work area

A full-size paper-marker footprint with checked sides, centers, and diagonals.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Tapes, notebooks, scrap-paper corner markers, approved removable tape if available; clear classroom floor.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A rectangular footprint must fit a proposed piece of equipment. Is checking two sides enough to establish a rectangle? Sketch a skewed counterexample.
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Inspect the available floor area and mark a datum corner. Use a classroom model footprint 36 × 48 in, or a teacher-approved smaller version.
  2. Mark the four corners with paper markers. Measure all four sides and both diagonals, keeping the tape straight.
  3. Write actual measurements; distinguish the intended 36/48 dimensions from your readings.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate the center and two proposed fixture points 12 in from the short ends along the middle line.
  2. Write coordinates from the same datum. Make a sketch another pair can follow.
  3. Use the ideal 60-in diagonal as a check for the 36 × 48 in model, not as a substitute for measuring the sides.

Worked example

For a 36 × 48 in rectangle, find the center and ideal diagonal. Center offsets = length/2 and width/2. Diagonal² = length² + width². Center = 18 in by 24 in from the datum. Diagonal = √(36² + 48²) = 60 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Lay out the center and fixture locations with small paper markers.
  2. A second pair measures all sides and diagonals independently.
  3. Adjust the corners and recheck after each move. Leave a before/after reading in the notebook.

Test · 20 min

  1. For this classroom exercise, sides must be within 1/4 in of the requested lengths and each diagonal within 1/4 in of 60 in; this is an assigned model tolerance.
  2. Show the datum, both fixture coordinates, and the final center.
  3. Explain why a matching pair of diagonals alone does not prove that the requested side dimensions are correct.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A new 30 × 40 in rectangle replaces the model. Give its center offsets and ideal diagonal. Which measurements would you repeat after moving a corner?
