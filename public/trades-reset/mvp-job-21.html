Job 21 · Revise an elevation budget

A revised route package after a finish or run-length change.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Prior service-bay drawings, paper strips, notebook, calculator.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A floor finish gets thicker. Which reference changes, and which dimensions must be recalculated?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Use a supplied model depth of 8 in above a fixed lower obstruction.
  2. Original finish occupies 1 in; route envelope 2 in; supports 1/2 in; route fall 2 in; required classroom gap 1 in.
  3. Create a stacked section showing all five allowances before changing anything.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Compute original and revised budgets independently.
  2. Mark the finish reference and any fixed obstruction explicitly.
  3. Propose a revised route only if a constraint is violated; a remaining positive budget alone does not verify every local crossing.

Worked example

Find spare depth, then increase finish thickness by 3/4 in. Spare = total depth − all occupied allowances. Original spare 8−(1+2+1/2+2+1)=1 1/2 in. New spare 3/4 in.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build both paper sections with colored allowance bands.
  2. Exchange the change order and ask another pair to reproduce the revised section.
  3. Check one local crossing and record whether it uses the same maximum envelope assumed in the budget.

Test · 20 min

  1. Every allowance appears once and uses the same direction/reference.
  2. Original and revised totals can be checked.
  3. Recommendation acknowledges bend, support, and crossing details beyond endpoint arithmetic.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A route runs 12 ft at 1/4 in/ft. The run increases to 16 ft at the same slope. How much extra fall is needed, and what happens to a depth budget with only 3/4 in spare?
