Job 26 · Build a low-voltage light circuit

A labeled circuit diagram and working switched lamp circuit, or a paper continuity model if parts are unavailable.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Teacher-selected low-voltage battery holder, matched lamp, insulated leads and switch; suitable meter if available; no wall-outlet wiring.

Equipment alternative: Build a large paper circuit diagram with a movable switch gap. Trace continuity and solve the supplied resistor cases; schedule the functioning-circuit practical when the kit is ready.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Trace a complete path from one battery terminal through the load and back. What does an open switch change?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Identify source rating, lamp rating, and connection points with the teacher.
  2. Before connecting power, draw a simple loop containing source, switch, and load.
  3. Use the teacher’s approved low-voltage kit; do not bridge source terminals or use unknown power supplies.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw symbols and label source voltage and expected open/closed states.
  2. Calculate the supplied resistor example, then explain what must be measured to compare a real circuit.
  3. Prepare a parts list and a connection check before energizing.

Worked example

A supplied resistor model has 6 V across 30 Ω. Find current. I=V/R. I=6/30=0.20 A. A real lamp may not have constant resistance as it warms; the resistor model is a calculation case.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build the approved source–switch–lamp loop with the source disconnected, then have the teacher inspect it.
  2. Connect the source and demonstrate three on/off cycles.
  3. If using a meter, follow the teacher’s selected mode and connection demonstration; record what quantity was actually measured.

Test · 20 min

  1. The diagram matches the physical circuit and switch interrupts the intended path.
  2. The lamp operates through three cycles without loose or hot connections.
  3. Paper alternative shows continuity decisions but is labeled untested electrically.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

For a supplied ideal 24-Ω resistor connected across 6 V, calculate current. Explain why an open switch stops current in the single-loop circuit.
