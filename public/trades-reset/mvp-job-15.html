Job 15 · Brace a frame

A paper frame that resists racking better after a documented revision.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Folded paper strips, tape, paper fasteners if available, notebooks, ruler/tape.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Push the top of a loose rectangle sideways in a sketch. Which dimension changes first, and why might a diagonal help?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Make a model frame 6 in wide and 8 in tall from stiff folded strips; use the same corner connections for both trials.
  2. Record both diagonals before pushing and mark a reference outline on paper.
  3. Choose a repeatable gentle displacement test: move the top corner 1 in sideways, release, and measure the remaining offset.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw the unbraced and proposed braced frames; mark load direction and connections.
  2. Calculate brace centerline length and add a stated overlap at both ends.
  3. Plan to compare remaining offset after the same initial displacement.

Worked example

A 6 × 8 in frame needs a corner-to-corner brace in a zero-thickness centerline model. Brace length = √(6² + 8²). 10 in between corner reference points; add measured attachment overlaps when making the actual strip.

Make · 28 min

  1. Test the unbraced frame gently; record residual offset.
  2. Add one diagonal brace with documented attachments. Repeat the same displacement test.
  3. Revise a weak connection and retest; do not claim the hand force was measured.

Test · 20 min

  1. The diagonal and attachment allowance are separately labeled.
  2. Before/after test conditions are comparable.
  3. Explanation mentions triangle geometry and connection behavior, using observed motion.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 9 × 12 in centerline rectangle needs a diagonal with 1 in attachment overlap at each end. Find brace blank length and explain why checking only the strip strength misses a possible failure.
