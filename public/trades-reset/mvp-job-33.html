Job 33 · Design and calculate

A coordinated plan, side view, and individual calculation sheet.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Approved survey, graph paper, paper model supplies, calculators.

Brief · 12 min

  1. What two views will let another person understand both footprint and height?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Review measured constraints and choose dimensions for the proposal.
  2. Distinguish specified design dimensions from measured existing conditions.
  3. Identify the governing clearance, span, slope, area, or power constraint for your project.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Each student completes one different project calculation with units and a reasonableness check.
  2. Coordinate plan and side view, adding material thickness/allowances where needed.
  3. Write dimensions another team needs to reproduce the model.

Worked example

An 18-in clear opening contains two 1/4-in partitions and three equal clear bays. Find each bay width. Clear bay width = (overall clear width − internal partition thicknesses)/bay count. (18−1/2)/3=5 5/6 in≈5.833 in. State a practical layout method and resolution rather than silently rounding all bays independently.

Make · 28 min

  1. Build a quick paper mockup from the drawing.
  2. Use it to detect one conflicting dimension or unclear connection.
  3. Revise drawing and calculations together; keep revision labels consistent.

Test · 20 min

  1. Two views agree.
  2. Math is traceable to source dimensions and assumptions.
  3. Each student explains how their calculation changes a design decision.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 20-in clear opening contains three 1/4-in partitions and four equal clear bays. Find the exact bay width. Name the controlling constraint in your project.
