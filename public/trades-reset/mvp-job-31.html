Job 31 · Write the improvement brief

A one-page proposal brief for a real school need with measurable acceptance criteria.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: School access, notebook, tape, paper; a staff-user need communicated through the teacher.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Choose a small need: organized equipment, a display, a storage layout, or a classroom arrangement. Who will use it and what would success look like?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Observe a teacher-approved location. Record facts separately from assumptions.
  2. Document at least three dimensions relevant to the need, with endpoints and units.
  3. Teacher supplies or gathers the user’s requirements; do not interrupt other classes to interview staff.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Write the user, problem, product, constraints, and three measurable acceptance checks.
  2. Calculate one early feasibility check from actual dimensions.
  3. Mark unknowns and assign how each will be verified.

Worked example

A model storage zone is 48 in wide. Three bins each need 14 in, with two 2-in gaps. Does the layout fit? Required width = bin widths + internal gaps. 3×14+2×2=46 in; 2 in remain for any stated outside margins.

Make · 28 min

  1. Make two quick paper concepts and compare them to the brief.
  2. Have another team challenge one assumption using the measured site.
  3. Choose a concept and revise the brief with the reason.

Test · 20 min

  1. Need is tied to a real observed context.
  2. Criteria can be checked by measurement or a stated practical test.
  3. Scope is a proposal/model; installation is not promised without school arrangements.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 55-in model zone must fit four 12-in bins and three 2-in gaps. Calculate required width and spare/conflict. Give one measurable criterion from your own brief.
