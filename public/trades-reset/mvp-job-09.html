Job 09 · Plan cuts from stock

A stock-cutting diagram that includes a stated cutting allowance.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper strips, scissors, tape measure, notebook; fictional stock lengths and prices.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Can total requested length divided by stock length always tell you how many pieces to buy? Try fitting two 5-ft pieces in 8-ft stock.
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Model 48-in stock as paper strips at 1 drawing inch = 4 real inches.
  2. Required parts: four 11 3/4-in pieces. For this planning exercise, each separating cut consumes 1/8 in of stock.
  3. Record stock length, part lengths, and the fictional allowance; scissors do not physically remove that exact kerf.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw the full stock strip with each part and each cut allowance.
  2. Write a cut sequence from one end and label usable leftover separately from cutting loss.
  3. Independently calculate the same plan in decimal inches, then compare.

Worked example

Can four 11 3/4-in parts fit in 48-in stock with four 1/8-in separating cuts? Used stock = sum of finished lengths + cut count × cutting allowance. 4×11 3/4 + 4×1/8 = 47 1/2 in; leftover = 1/2 in. Count the final separating cut because unused stock remains.

Make · 28 min

  1. Cut paper parts using the scaled plan; mark each allowance band rather than pretending scissors have a 1/8-in kerf.
  2. Trade the diagram with a partner and ask them to locate each finished piece.
  3. Test a different grouping of requested lengths on a fresh strip.

Test · 20 min

  1. Finished lengths, number of separating cuts, kerf loss, and leftover sum to stock length.
  2. No finished part is shorter than required.
  3. Paper model and stock diagram use matching scales.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

Three 15 3/4-in parts come from 48-in stock. Assume three 1/8-in separating cuts. Find used length and leftover.
