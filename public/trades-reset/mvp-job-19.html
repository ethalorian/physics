Job 19 · Lay out a sloping route

A dry paper route with calculated fall and a verified elevation drawing.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Tape, notebook, paper, low supports, checked level; no water required.

Equipment alternative: Use a dimensioned drawing and build a scale paper elevation; mark reference data supplied and reserve instrument evidence for a later practical.

Brief · 12 min

  1. A route drops 1/4 in per foot. Does a longer route need the same total drop?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Use a classroom design brief: a 6-ft horizontal run at 1/4 in fall per foot. This is a math model, not a prescribed installation rule.
  2. Build at 1 drawing inch=1 actual foot and record horizontal/vertical scale clearly.
  3. Establish the starting elevation and available low-model space.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate end elevation relative to the start.
  2. Draw plan and elevation; label which length is horizontal run rather than sloped path.
  3. Choose whether the elevation uses an enlarged vertical scale; label it if different.

Worked example

Find fall and percent slope for 6 ft at 1/4 in per foot. Fall = run × fall per foot. Percent slope = vertical fall/horizontal run ×100 with equal units. Fall 1 1/2 in. Run 72 in; slope 1.5/72×100 ≈2.08%.

Make · 28 min

  1. Construct the dry paper route on low supports.
  2. Check endpoint difference with a level reference, or compare the supplied elevation drawing if equipment is unavailable.
  3. Adjust a support and record the resulting fall.

Test · 20 min

  1. Slope calculation uses matching units.
  2. Both drawing scales are stated.
  3. Physical or drawing inspection distinguishes a local sag from total endpoint fall.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

An 8-ft horizontal run drops 2 in. Find in/ft and percent slope. At the same in/ft, what fall does 10 ft require?
