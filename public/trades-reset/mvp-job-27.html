Job 27 · Compare series and parallel paths

Two circuit layouts with a path comparison and resistor-model calculations.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Approved low-voltage kit with two matched lamps and source; or colored paper circuit paths.

Equipment alternative: Use paper branch paths and the supplied ideal resistor cases. Record no invented voltage/current measurements.

Brief · 12 min

  1. If one load’s connection opens, when will another load still have a complete path?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Draw one series loop and two parallel branches using the same component symbols.
  2. Identify which components share current in series and which share voltage in parallel.
  3. Use an ideal calculation case with two equal 12-Ω resistors and a 6-V source.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate ideal branch/total currents and annotate both drawings.
  2. Predict what happens if one connection opens in each arrangement.
  3. Separate ideal resistor predictions from observations of real lamps.

Worked example

Compare the supplied equal-resistor cases. Series Rtotal=R 1+R 2. Parallel branches each receive source voltage. Series 24 Ω gives total 0.25 A. Parallel: each branch 6/12=0.5 A; total 1 A. Use only kit combinations approved for the source.

Make · 28 min

  1. With power disconnected, construct each teacher-approved configuration.
  2. Observe operation and the effect of opening one branch connection after disconnecting power to rearrange.
  3. Record actual behavior or trace paper continuity if using the alternative.

Test · 20 min

  1. Diagram and construction agree.
  2. The explanation follows complete paths rather than saying electricity chooses only one route.
  3. Total and branch currents are labeled distinctly in the supplied math.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

Two equal 20-Ω ideal resistors use a 4-V source. Find series total current, parallel branch current, and parallel total current.
