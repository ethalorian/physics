Job 36 · Defend and hand over the proposal

A complete project package and individual response to a changed requirement.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Brief, survey, drawings, math, estimate, prototype, test/revision record; notebook.

Brief · 12 min

  1. What evidence would make the user trust your proposal enough to consider the next step?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Lay out the brief, checked survey, current drawings, takeoff/quote, and test record in order.
  2. Identify one strong piece of evidence and one remaining uncertainty.
  3. Each student selects a measurement, calculation, and revision they can personally explain.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Prepare a three-minute demonstration: need, design, math, evidence, limitation, next step.
  2. Check that all pages use the same revision and units.
  3. Anticipate a changed dimension, material size, or budget and plan how to re-evaluate it.

Worked example

A fictional $80 materials estimate increases 15% after a change. Find revised total and increase. Increase = original×0.15; revised = original+increase. $12 increase; revised $92. A percentage allowance needs a stated reason; it does not replace a revised takeoff.

Make · 28 min

  1. Present the proposal and demonstrate one acceptance check.
  2. Each student independently solves the teacher’s change card before team discussion.
  3. Revise the package after feedback and provide a clear recommendation: ready for further planning, revise, or needs more evidence.

Test · 20 min

  1. Package is usable by someone who did not attend the build.
  2. Every student defends a calculation and physical decision individually.
  3. Unknowns and any unperformed real-system tests remain visible; no installation promise is implied.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

Independently: a proposal costs $120 before a stated 10% classroom contingency. Find total. Then name one actual project change that requires new measurements rather than only multiplying the old estimate.
