Job 32 · Survey for the proposal

A checked site survey and a list of unresolved measurements.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Tapes, notebooks, graph/loose paper; approved school zone.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Which missing measurement could make your proposal fail even if all arithmetic is correct?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Establish a datum and orientation for the selected project.
  2. Measure boundaries, relevant feature offsets, and operating/access space at reachable locations.
  3. Repeat two critical dimensions independently; keep both readings and note discrepancies.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Prepare a dimensioned NTS survey or a scale plan with a labeled scale.
  2. Use a dimension-string check against a direct overall measurement.
  3. Create an unknowns list rather than guessing inaccessible dimensions.

Worked example

A width reads 47 3/4 in from one pass and 47 7/8 in from another. Find disagreement and decide what to do before specifying a tight fit. Disagreement = absolute difference between readings. 1/8 in. Recheck endpoints, tape alignment, and required clearance; averaging alone does not establish the true fit.

Make · 28 min

  1. Give the survey to another team for a reconstruction on paper.
  2. They identify every ambiguous endpoint or missing feature.
  3. Revisit the site within teacher-approved access and issue a corrected survey.

Test · 20 min

  1. Critical features can be located from the drawing.
  2. Checks and unresolved dimensions are visible.
  3. Every student performs and records at least one actual measurement.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A 120-in overall dimension contains 38 1/2 in, 44 3/4 in, and one missing segment. Find the missing segment. Identify your project’s most important measurement check.
