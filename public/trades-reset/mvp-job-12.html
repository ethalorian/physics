Job 12 · Deliver a buildable job package

A drawing, stock plan, estimate, and paper model another pair can build.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper, scissors, tape, tapes, notebooks; prior work orders.

Brief · 12 min

  1. Choose one previous layout or template. What information did the builder still have to ask for?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Select a 12 × 18 in tabletop organizer footprint or teacher-approved equivalent.
  2. Use two dividers running the 12-in direction and spaced equally across the 18-in direction; divider thickness is neglected in the initial paper model.
  3. Record the user need, dimensions, assumptions, and a datum.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Draw plan and elevation, including divider height of your choice with units.
  2. Prepare a paper cut list and a simulated quote using $0.10 per sheet and $0.02 per 6-in tape segment.
  3. Calculate material needs from a piece layout, rounding purchases up.

Worked example

Two divider centerlines split an 18-in length into three equal bays in a zero-thickness model. Locate them. Three bays → three intervals; interval = 18/3. Centers at 6 and 12 in from datum. Real divider thickness would reduce clear bay width.

Make · 28 min

  1. Give the package to another pair; they build the paper organizer without seeing your model.
  2. Record every clarification the builder requests.
  3. Revise the package and have the builder demonstrate how the revision resolves one uncertainty.

Test · 20 min

  1. Model agrees with the specified footprint and divider positions within a stated classroom tolerance.
  2. The cut list and quote match the model.
  3. Each student independently explains one calculation and one limitation of the paper model.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A revised zero-thickness organizer has a 24-in length divided into four equal bays. How many internal dividers are needed, and where are their centerlines? Name a package revision supported by your builder’s feedback.
