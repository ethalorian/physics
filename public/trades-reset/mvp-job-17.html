Job 17 · Spread the bearing load

A bearing-area comparison and improved paper support assembly.

110 minutes • Tape measure + pocket notebook • Name: __________________ Date: __________

Materials: Paper support columns with narrow and wide paper feet, soft scrap paper pads, small equal loads; balance or force meter optional.

Brief · 12 min

  1. The same load rests on a narrow or wide foot. What quantity connects load and contact area?
  2. Set up a dated notebook spread: sketch and readings on the left; calculation, decision, test, and revision on the right. Mark small sketches NTS unless drawn to a chosen scale.
  3. Work in pairs and rotate roles. Each student keeps an individual notebook and performs a tool action and a calculation.

Measure · 18 min

  1. Make two support feet with different measured contact areas: model sizes 1 ×1 in and 1 ×2 in.
  2. Keep load, column height, and pad material the same.
  3. Record whether load is known in newtons or only as a count of equal objects.

Plan · 20 min

  1. Calculate both paper foot areas; predict the relative average pressure under the same load.
  2. Draw load arrows from top to foot and label contact area.
  3. Plan a pad-mark or tilt comparison; acknowledge that visible deformation is a proxy rather than a pressure gauge.

Worked example

A supplied example load is 12 N on contact areas 2 cm² and 6 cm². Compare average pressure. Average pressure = force/contact area. 6 N/cm² versus 2 N/cm². Tripling area reduces this average pressure to one third for the same force.

Make · 28 min

  1. Test the two feet with the same small load over the desk.
  2. Record sinking, imprint, tilt, or lack of visible difference honestly.
  3. Build an improved support by changing foot area or connection; test again.

Test · 20 min

  1. Area calculations use square units.
  2. No newton value is invented from unknown object counts.
  3. Conclusion distinguishes the pressure model from the observed pad response.

Explain · 12 min

  1. Complete the individual exit below before discussing answers.
  2. Add the test result, revision, and notebook page reference to your response. Use the notebook as evidence rather than retyping every page.
  3. Save the three selected responses and submit the lesson for teacher review. Show your physical work and notebook to the teacher. Clean up and return shared supplies.

Individual exit

A supplied 20-N force acts over 5 cm². Find average pressure. What area gives 2 N/cm² under the same force?
