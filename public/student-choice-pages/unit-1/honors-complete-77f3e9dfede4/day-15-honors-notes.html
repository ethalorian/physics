D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A rope lifts a 2 kg lamp with upward acceleration 1.5 m/s². Find the tension. Use g = 9.8 m/s².
GIVEN
m = 2 kg
ay = +1.5 m/s²
g = 9.8 m/s²
EQUATION
Up is +.
T − mg = may.
WORK
W = 2(9.8) = 19.6 N
T = 19.6 + 2(1.5)
ANSWER
T = 22.6 N
T and W are not a third-law pair.
