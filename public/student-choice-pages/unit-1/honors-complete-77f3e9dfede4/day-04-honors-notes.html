D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A cart moves at +3 m/s for 2 s, then −1 m/s for 2 s. Find its displacement and total distance.
GIVEN
v₁ = +3 m/s
v₂ = −1 m/s
2 s per interval
EQUATION
Use areas because each interval has steady v.
WORK
Δx = 3(2) − 1(2)
distance = 6 + 2
ANSWER
+4 m; 8 m
Return travel reduces Δx, not distance.
