SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A 4 kg cart has forces of 12 N right and 4 N left. Its initial velocity is −3 m/s. Find a and v after 1 s.
GIVEN
m = 4 kg
Fright = 12 N; Fleft = 4 N
vi = −3 m/s
EQUATION
Sum forces first.
ΣFx = max.
v = vi + axt.
WORK
ΣFx = 12 − 4 = +8 N
ax = 8/4 = +2 m/s²
v = −3 + 2(1)
ANSWER
v = −1 m/s
Still leftward; speed is now 1 m/s.
