SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A 3 kg cart has 9 N right and 3 N left. Someone writes a = 9/3 = 3 m/s². Explain and repair the first wrong step.
GIVEN
m = 3 kg
right is +
forces: +9 N and −3 N
EQUATION
Use net force, not the largest force.
WORK
ΣFx = 9 − 3 = +6 N
ax = 6/3 = +2 m/s²
ANSWER
+2 m/s²
Check: ma = 6 N.
