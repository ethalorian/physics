Velocity
LEARNING TARGET · S1
I can use a position–time graph’s slope to find velocity.

SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Velocity
How fast and which way. The rate position changes.
Speed
How fast only. The size of the velocity, with no direction.
Slope
Steepness of a line: rise ÷ run. On a position–time graph, slope = velocity.
Average speed
Total distance ÷ time. A scalar — no direction. Velocity adds the sign.

SECTION B KEY EQUATIONS + UNITS

v = Δx ÷ Δt
speed = distance ÷ time
v
m/s (vector: has a sign)
convert
1 m/s = 3.6 km/h

SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A cart goes from 8 m to 2 m in 3 s. Right is positive. Find its velocity.
GIVEN
xi = 8 m
xf = 2 m
Δt = 3 s
EQUATION
v = Δx ÷ Δt
WORK
Δx = 2 − 8 = −6 m
v = −6 m ÷ 3 s
ANSWER
−2 m/s
2 m/s to the left
