SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Force
A push or pull from an interaction with another object.
Net force
The combined effect of all forces on one object.
Newton’s 1st law
1st law: at rest stays at rest; moving keeps its velocity — unless ΣF ≠ 0.
Inertia
An object keeps its velocity unless a net force changes it. More mass, more inertia.

SECTION B KEY IDEA + UNITS
