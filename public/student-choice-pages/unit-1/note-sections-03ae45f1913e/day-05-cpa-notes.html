CPA PHYSICS · UNIT 1 · DAY 5
Predicting position and uncertainty
LEARNING TARGET · R3
I can use velocity and time to predict where it will be, including the possible error.

SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Starting position
Where the object is at t = 0. It is often not zero.
Change
Velocity × time: how far the position shifts.
Final position
Start + change.
Prediction range
A ± bound. “10 ± 2 m” means 8 m to 12 m. More time usually means a wider range.

SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A cart starts at 8 m and moves at −2 m/s for 3 s. Where is it at the end?
GIVEN
xi = 8 m
v = −2 m/s
t = 3 s
EQUATION
xf = xi + v × t
WORK
change = (−2)(3) = −6 m
xf = 8 − 6
ANSWER
2 m
moved 6 m left
