HONORS PHYSICS · UNIT 1 · DAY 3
Velocity and position–time graphs
LEARNING TARGET · S1
I can calculate average velocity and interpret a position–time graph using slope.

SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Average velocity
v̄ = Δx ÷ Δt. Carries direction; summarizes a whole interval.
Average speed
Total distance ÷ elapsed time. No direction.
Slope
On an x–t graph, slope = velocity (units m/s). Height = position, not velocity.
Instantaneous velocity
Velocity at one moment: the tangent slope on a curved x–t graph.

SECTION B KEY EQUATION + UNITS

v̄ = Δx ÷ Δt
Δx = xf − xi
v
velocity, meters per second (m/s)
speed
total distance ÷ Δt

SECTION C COLOR IT · READ A GRAPH AS A MOTION STORY

Height = position. Slope = velocity.
1 · PURPLE
Rising: moving +, v > 0
2 · PURPLE
Flat: stopped, v = 0
3 · PURPLE
Falling: moving −, v < 0

SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A straight x–t line passes through (2 s, 14 m) and (6 s, 6 m). Find the velocity, then predict x at 8 s.
GIVEN
(2 s, 14 m)
(6 s, 6 m)
predict at 8 s
EQUATION
v = Δx ÷ Δt
x = xi + v t
WORK
v = (6 − 14) ÷ (6 − 2)
x(8) = 6 + (−2)(2)
ANSWER
−2 m/s; 2 m
if the line continues
