SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Inertial frame
An unforced object keeps its velocity.
Net force
Vector sum of external forces on a system.
Inertia
More mass resists velocity change more.
Equilibrium
Net force zero; v constant, not always zero.

SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A cart is at −2 m, moving at +3 m/s. Its external forces balance for the next 4 s. Find its final position.
GIVEN
x0 = −2 m
vi = +3 m/s
ΣF = 0; t = 4 s
EQUATION
First law: constant v.
xf = x0 + vt.
WORK
a = 0 m/s²
xf = −2 + 3(4)
ANSWER
+10 m
Net force zero; motion continues.
