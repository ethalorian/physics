Equilibrium: find the missing balance
LEARNING TARGET
I can find a missing force when acceleration is zero.

SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Equilibrium
Acceleration is zero. The forces sum to zero in every direction.
Two kinds
At rest or moving at constant velocity.
Missing force
Whatever is needed to make each direction balance.
Careful
Zero velocity for an instant does not guarantee equilibrium.
