CPA PHYSICS · UNIT 1 · DAY 17
Friction: before and during sliding
LEARNING TARGET
I can distinguish adjustable static friction from kinetic friction.

SECTION A KEY VOCABULARY

Static friction
Before sliding. Adjusts to match the pull, up to a limit.
Maximum static
The greatest static friction possible. Pull past it and sliding starts.
Kinetic friction
During sliding. Use the supplied value.
Coefficient (μ)
When given: kinetic friction = μ × normal force.

SECTION B KEY EQUATIONS + UNITS

at rest: fs = pull (≤ limit)
sliding: fk = μ × FN

SECTION D WORKED EXAMPLE

A 2 kg crate slides right. Pull = 10 N right; kinetic friction = 4 N left. Find its acceleration.
GIVEN
m = 2 kg
pull 10 N right
fk 4 N left
EQUATION
a = Fnet ÷ m
WORK
Fnet = 10 − 4 = 6 N right
a = 6 ÷ 2
ANSWER
3 m/s² right
