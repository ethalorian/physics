D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A 2 kg system slides with 0.4 N of opposing friction. Compare acceleration with driving forces of 2 N and 4 N.
GIVEN
M = 2 kg
f = 0.4 N
Fdrive = 2 N, then 4 N
EQUATION
Subtract friction.
Then a = Fnet/M.
WORK
a1 = (2 − 0.4)/2 = 0.8
a2 = (4 − 0.4)/2 = 1.8
ANSWER
0.8; 1.8 m/s²
Driving force doubles; a does not.
