D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A car moving at +20 m/s brakes at −4 m/s² until it stops. Find its stopping distance, then check the result.
GIVEN
vi = +20 m/s
vf = 0 m/s
a = −4 m/s²
EQUATION
No time given:
vf² = vi² + 2aΔx.
WORK
0 = 400 − 8Δx
t = (0 − 20)/(−4)
area = ½(20)(5)
ANSWER
Δx = +50 m
5 s to stop; area also gives 50 m.
