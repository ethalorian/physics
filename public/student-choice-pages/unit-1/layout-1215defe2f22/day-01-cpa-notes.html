A · KEY VOCABULARY
Observation
Something we record or measure, like a position at a time.
Model
A useful “if”: if the motion keeps going the same way, we can continue the pattern.
Prediction
An estimate of what comes next, made by continuing the pattern.
Testable question
One that measurements can answer. “Where will it be tomorrow?” — yes. “Who should pay for a mission?” — no; that also needs human decisions.

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A cart is at 2 m, 3 m, then 4 m, one second apart. Predict its position one second later.
GIVEN
x = 2, 3, 4 m
1 s between readings
EQUATION
next = last + change each second
WORK
change = 3 − 2 = +1 m
next = 4 m + 1 m
ANSWER
5 m
if the motion continues the same way
