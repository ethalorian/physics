D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A 4 kg cart has 12 N pulling right and 4 N of friction left. Find the acceleration.
GIVEN
m = 4 kg
12 N right
4 N left
EQUATION
a = Fnet ÷ m
WORK
Fnet = 12 − 4 = 8 N right
a = 8 ÷ 4
ANSWER
2 m/s² right
with the net force
