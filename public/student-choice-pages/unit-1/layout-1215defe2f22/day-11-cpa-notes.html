D · WORKED EXAMPLE
In one trial, velocity rises from 0 to 4 m/s in 2 s. Find the acceleration.
GIVEN
vi = 0 m/s
vf = 4 m/s
Δt = 2 s
EQUATION
a = Δv ÷ Δt
WORK
Δv = 4 − 0 = 4 m/s
a = 4 ÷ 2
ANSWER
2 m/s²
