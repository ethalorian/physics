Forces: what pushes and pulls
LEARNING TARGET · K2
I can name the forces in a scenario: gravity, normal, friction, applied, tension.
A · KEY VOCABULARY
Interaction
Every force has a source (what pushes or pulls) and a receiver.
Contact force
Normal (surface, ⟂), friction (along surface, opposes slip), tension (rope), applied (a push).
Non-contact force
Gravity acts without touching.
Mass vs. weight
Mass is amount of matter (kg). Weight is Earth’s gravitational pull (N).
B · KEY EQUATION + UNITS
Fg = m g
g = 9.8 N/kg (≈ 10 to estimate)
m
mass, kilograms (kg)
W
weight, newtons (N)

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A 3 kg object rests on a level table. Find its weight and name the force that supports it.
GIVEN
m = 3 kg
g ≈ 10 N/kg
EQUATION
W = m × g
WORK
W = 3 × 10 = 30 N down
table pushes up equally
ANSWER
30 N
normal force 30 N up
