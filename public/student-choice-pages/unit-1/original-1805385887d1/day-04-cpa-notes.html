Velocity–time graph

Flat v–t line
Steady velocity — not necessarily stopped.
Area under the line
Height × width = displacement. Area below zero is negative displacement.
Components
The separate east and north changes that make up one diagonal displacement.

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
Velocity is +3 m/s for 2 s. Find the displacement and name its direction.
GIVEN
v = +3 m/s
t = 2 s
EQUATION
Δx = v × t
WORK
Δx = (3 m/s)(2 s)
ANSWER
+6 m
6 m to the right
