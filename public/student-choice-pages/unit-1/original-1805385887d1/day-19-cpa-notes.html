Workshop: find and fix the reasoning
LEARNING TARGET

A · KEY VOCABULARY
Distance vs. displacement
Trace the whole path vs. join start to finish.
Net force first
Combine all forces before dividing by mass.
Sign = direction
A minus sign is not “slow.” Compare sizes for speed.
Pair forces
Act on different objects, so they never cancel.

C · COLOR IT · FOUR COMMON MISTAKES
Lightly shade each strip.
1CLAIM ✗ “3 m right then 3 m back — the distance is zero.”
2FIX ✓ Distance = 3 + 3 = 6 m. Displacement is 0 m: the endpoints match.
1CLAIM ✗ “8 N pull on a 2 kg cart: 8 ÷ 2 = 4 m/s².”
2FIX ✓ Net force first: 8 − 2 = 6 N. Then 6 ÷ 2 = 3 m/s².
1CLAIM ✗ “−2 → −6 m/s is slowing, because it’s negative.”
2FIX ✓ Speed grows from 2 to 6 m/s: speeding up. The minus sign is direction.
1CLAIM ✗ “The force pair cancels, so nothing moves.”
2FIX ✓ Each pair force acts on a different cart. Draw one on each diagram.

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A 3 kg cart has 9 N right and 3 N left. A student writes 9 ÷ 3 = 3 m/s². Correct it.
GIVEN
m = 3 kg
9 N right
3 N left
EQUATION
a = Fnet ÷ m
WORK
Fnet = 9 − 3 = 6 N right
a = 6 ÷ 3
ANSWER
2 m/s² right
missed: net force first
