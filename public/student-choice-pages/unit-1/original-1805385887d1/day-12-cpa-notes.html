Proportional
Double the net force → double the acceleration.
Friction offset
Friction lowers the net force, so real data can sit below the ideal line.

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
In an ideal 2 kg system, 2 N gives 1 m/s². Predict the acceleration at 4 N with the same mass.
GIVEN
F = 2 N → a = 1 m/s²
new F = 4 N
same mass
EQUATION
a ∝ F (same mass)
WORK
4 N ÷ 2 N = 2× the force
a = 2 × 1 m/s²
ANSWER
2 m/s²
acceleration doubles
