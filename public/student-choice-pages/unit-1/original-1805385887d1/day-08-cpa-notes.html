LEARNING TARGET · R5
I can choose and use an equation for motion with steady acceleration.
A · KEY VOCABULARY
Steady velocity
Equal gaps; velocity does not change. Use v × t.
Constant acceleration
Velocity changes by the same amount each second.
Average velocity
(start + final) ÷ 2 — only when acceleration is constant.
Method
A choice with a reason: “I chose ___ because the velocity ___.”

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A cart speeds up steadily from 0 to 8 m/s in 4 s. Find its displacement.
GIVEN
vi = 0, vf = 8 m/s
t = 4 s
constant a
EQUATION
Δx = ½(vi + vf) × t
WORK
average = (0 + 8) ÷ 2 = 4 m/s
Δx = 4 × 4
ANSWER
+16 m
average works: a is constant
