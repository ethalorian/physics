Position predictions and their limits
LEARNING TARGET · R3
I can predict a position and state its uncertainty.
A · KEY VOCABULARY
Start x0
Position when the chosen interval begins.
Bound
An allowed range, not a standard deviation.
Half-width
The ± value; full width is twice as large.
Assumption
A condition treated as true, such as steady v.
B · KEY EQUATIONS + UNITS
xf = x0 + vt
δxf = δx0 + tδv
t
exact time, s
δx
bound, m

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A cart starts at 8 ± 0.5 m and moves at −2.0 ± 0.1 m/s for an exact 3 s. Find its predicted position interval.
GIVEN
x0 = 8 ± 0.5 m
v = −2.0 ± 0.1 m/s
t = 3 s; steady v
EQUATION
Steady v: xf = x0 + vt.
Add input bounds.
WORK
xf = 8 − 2(3)
δx = .5 + 3(.1)
= 0.8 m
ANSWER
1.2 to 2.8 m
2.0 ± 0.8 m; full width 1.6 m.
