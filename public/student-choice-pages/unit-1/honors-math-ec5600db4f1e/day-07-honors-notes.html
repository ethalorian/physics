D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A cart's velocity changes uniformly from +6 m/s to −2 m/s in 4 s. Find its acceleration and the time it reverses.
GIVEN
vi = +6 m/s
vf = −2 m/s
Δt = 4 s
EQUATION
a = Δv/Δt.
Turn: 0 = vi + at.
WORK
a = (−2 − 6)/4
0 = 6 − 2t
t = 3 s
ANSWER
a = −2 m/s²
Slows for 3 s, then speeds up to the left.
