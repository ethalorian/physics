LEARNING TARGET · R9
I can use a force diagram to find acceleration on a ramp.
A · KEY VOCABULARY
Parallel ∥
Along the ramp; choose downhill as +.
Normal axis ⊥
Straight out of the ramp, usually not vertical.
Weight parts
mg sin θ along; mg cos θ into the ramp.
Constraint
No motion through the surface: a⊥ = 0.
B · KEY EQUATIONS + UNITS
ma∥ = mg sin θ − f
N = mg cos θ

D · WORKED EXAMPLE
A 2 kg block slides down a 30° ramp with 2 N friction uphill. Find its acceleration and normal force. Use g = 9.8 m/s².
GIVEN
m = 2 kg; θ = 30°
f = 2 N uphill
a⊥ = 0
EQUATION
mg sin θ − f = ma∥
N = mg cos θ
WORK
a∥ = [19.6(0.5) − 2]/2
N = 19.6 cos 30°
ANSWER
3.9 m/s²
Downhill; N ≈ 17.0 N.
