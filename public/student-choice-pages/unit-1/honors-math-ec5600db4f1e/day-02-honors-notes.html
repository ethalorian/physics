HONORS PHYSICS · UNIT 1 · DAY 2
Where are you? How far did you travel?
LEARNING TARGET
I can describe a route using an origin, signed position, total distance, and displacement.
A · KEY VOCABULARY
Position
A signed coordinate in a reference frame: choose an origin and a + direction. x = −2 m is 2 m left of the origin.
Distance
Sum of the length of every leg: |x2 − x1| + |x3 − x2| + … Never negative.
Displacement
Δx = xf − xi. Its sign depends on the axis you chose; the physical motion does not.
Scalar / vector
A scalar has magnitude + units (distance, time). A vector also has direction (displacement).
