WORKED EXAMPLE — TOGETHER FIRST

The 10 m Balcony

A coin is dropped from rest off a 10 m balcony. How long until it hits the ground? (Ignore air.)

GIVEN  y = 10 m · v0 = 0 · g = 9.81 m/s² ASKED  t = ? EQUATION  y = ½·g·t²
10 = ½·(9.81)·t² t² = 20 / 9.81 ≈ 2.04 t ≈ 1.43 s about a second and a half — try it against a stopwatch.

VOCAB · FREE FALL — WATCH OUT

Free Fall ≠ Floating

WHAT IT MEANS

Motion under gravity only — no air resistance, no other forces. Falling IS the motion.

WHY IT MATTERS LATER

Astronauts "float" because they're in free fall. Hold that thought until Day 6 — and Day 13.
