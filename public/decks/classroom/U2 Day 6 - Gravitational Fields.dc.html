DIRECT INSTRUCTION — THE FIELD MOVE

A Number at Every
Point in Space

Divide the force by the mass that feels it, and what's left doesn't belong to any object — it belongs to space itself.

g(r) is waiting at every point around Earth, whether or not anything is there to feel it. N/kg and m/s² are the same unit.

THE DERIVATION — THREE LINES
F = G·M·m / r²
F / m = G·M / r²  ← divide both sides by m
g(r) = G·M / r²  ← name it: field strength

WORKED EXAMPLE — THE CHECK

Does It Give 9.81?

Compute g at Earth's surface from M and R alone. If the field idea is right, a familiar number should fall out.

GIVEN  M = 5.97×10²⁴ kg · R = 6.37×10⁶ m ASKED  g(R) = ? EQUATION  g = G·M / R²
g = (3.98×10¹⁴) / (4.06×10¹³) g ≈ 9.81 m/s² the number we've measured all along — predicted from Earth's mass and size. The equation works.
