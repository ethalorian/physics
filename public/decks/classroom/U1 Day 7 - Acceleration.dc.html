Rate of Change of Velocity

a = Δv / Δt
UNITS: m/s² — "EACH SECOND, YOUR VELOCITY CHANGES BY a METERS PER SECOND"
the car: 27 m/s ÷ 6 s ≈ 4.5 m/s²
free fall: 9.8 m/s² — the car pulls half a g
Speeding up → a points the way you're moving.
Slowing down → a points against your motion. Negative sign, real meaning.
Turning at constant speed → still accelerating. Velocity is a vector.
