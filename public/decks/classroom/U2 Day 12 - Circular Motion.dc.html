WORKED EXAMPLE — TOGETHER FIRST

The Car on the Curve

A 1500 kg car rounds a 50 m curve at 20 m/s. How much centripetal force must friction provide?

GIVEN  m = 1500 kg · v = 20 m/s · r = 50 m ASKED  Fc = ? EQUATION  Fc = m·v² / r
Fc = 1500 × 20² / 50 = 1500 × 400 / 50 Fc = 12,000 N inward friction supplies ALL of it. If the road can't — the car goes tangent, exactly like the cut string.
