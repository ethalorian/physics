THE DERIVATION — GRAVITY MEETS CENTRIPETAL

Force Them
to Agree

For a circular orbit, gravity IS the centripetal force. Set them equal and solve for v — watch what cancels.

Check it at ISS altitude: v ≈ 7,700 m/s — the exact speed from yesterday's worked example. The two 8.7s were never a coincidence.

WORKED — EVERY STEP
G·M·m / r² = m·v² / r  ← set Fg = Fc
G·M / r² = v² / r  ← m cancels!
G·M / r = v²  ← multiply by r
vorbit = √(G·M / r)
only the central mass and the radius. The satellite's mass never mattered.
