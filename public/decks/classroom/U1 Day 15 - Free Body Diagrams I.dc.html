Six Rules, One Picture

1Draw just the object — a dot or a box.
2One arrow per force acting on it.
3Arrows point in the force's direction.
4Length proportional to magnitude.
5Label every arrow: Fgravity, Fnormal, Ffriction…
6Only forces ON the object — never the ones it exerts on other things.
