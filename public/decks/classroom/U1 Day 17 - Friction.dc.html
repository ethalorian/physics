Two Frictions

STATIC — BEFORE IT MOVES
fs ≤ μs · N

Adjusts itself to match your push — up to a ceiling. Cross it, and the box breaks loose.

KINETIC — WHILE IT SLIDES
fk = μk · N

Steady while sliding — and usually smaller than the static ceiling. That's your Do Now answer.

BOTH DEPEND ON: THE NORMAL FORCE PRESSING THE SURFACES TOGETHER · THE SURFACE PAIR (μ) — NOT THE SPEED, NOT THE AREA
