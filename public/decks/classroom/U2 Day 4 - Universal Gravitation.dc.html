EQUATION · ANATOMY

Fgravitational force between the two masses (newtons)
G6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² — always the same. That's why it's universal.
m1 m2the two masses (kg) — either can be a planet, a moon, or you
r²distance between centers, squared. Double r → F drops to ¼.
⚠ r is between CENTERS, not surfaces. G is tiny — that's why you don't feel your neighbor's gravity.

THE GEOMETRY BEHIND THE LAW

Why Squared?

Gravity spreads out over the surface of a sphere — and a sphere's surface grows as r².

At twice the distance, the same influence covers four times the area — so each point gets a quarter of it. Not half. A quarter.
