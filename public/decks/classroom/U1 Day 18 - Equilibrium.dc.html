a = 0 Means the Forces Balance

CASE 1 · AT REST — BOOK ON DESK
ΣFvertical = 0 → N = Fgravity

1 kg book: Fgravity = 9.8 N, so the desk pushes up with exactly 9.8 N. The desk "knows."

CASE 2 · CONSTANT VELOCITY — PUSHED BOX
ΣFhorizontal = 0 → Fapplied = f

Pushing at 20 N and it glides steadily? Then friction is exactly 20 N — regardless of the box's mass.
