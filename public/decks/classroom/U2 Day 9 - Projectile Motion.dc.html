EQUATION · ANATOMY — INDEPENDENT MOTION

Gravity Only Gets One Axis

HORIZONTAL — UNTOUCHED

x = vx · t

No horizontal force → vx never changes. Unit 1's inertia, sideways.

VERTICAL — FREE FALL

y = y0 − ½·g·t²

Gravity lives here. vy grows by 9.8 m/s every second of the fall.

EVERY STEP, SAME STORY

vx — same arrow every step.
vy — grows every step.

The two pieces never talk. Time is the only thing they share.

WORKED EXAMPLE — TOGETHER FIRST

Off the 5 m Cliff

Fired horizontally at vx = 10 m/s from a 5 m cliff. (a) How long in the air? (b) How far out does it land?

GIVEN  h = 5 m · vx = 10 m/s · g = 9.81 m/s² ASKED  t, then x ORDER  (1) h = ½·g·t²   (2) x = vx·t
5 = ½·(9.81)·t² t = √(2·5 / 9.81) ≈ 1.01 s x = 10 × 1.01 x ≈ 10.1 m time from the VERTICAL. Distance from the HORIZONTAL. Never mixed.
