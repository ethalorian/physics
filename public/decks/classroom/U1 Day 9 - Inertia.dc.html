A 2,000-Year Argument

ARISTOTLE — WRONG, FOR 2,000 YEARS

"Objects naturally come to rest." Feels true — everything you've ever pushed eventually stops.

GALILEO — RIGHT, BY THOUGHT EXPERIMENT

"They stop because something stops them." Remove the something, and motion continues forever.

NEWTON'S 1st LAW

An object in motion stays in motion, and an object at rest stays at rest — unless acted on by a net force.

Good news: it's predictable. Bad news: it won't stop on its own. To change its path, someone has to apply a force.
