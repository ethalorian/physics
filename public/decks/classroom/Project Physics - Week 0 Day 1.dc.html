Our fictional mission

2026-XJ is a fictional asteroid with an uncertain path. This class is the engineering team: **predict, measure, compare and revise**. The full briefing distinguishes measurements, estimates and unknowns. Today’s paper-drop test practices that same evidence habit. Our four mission phases build the tools to test increasingly demanding claims.

Four mission phases

## Four mission phases 1. **Track it — Motion, forces, structures.** Measure motion and test a design. 2. **Move it — Energy.** Account for energy used to do a job. 3. **Sense it — Fluids, waves, electromagnetism.** Gather evidence through physical signals. 4. **Power it, prove it — Generation and capstone.** Build, measure performance and defend the design. Our routine: predict, build, measure, explain.
