DIRECT INSTRUCTION — THE DISTINCTION THAT RUNS THE DAY

Two Words, Two Physics

MASS · kg · masa

How much stuff. Same on Earth, on the Moon, on 2026-XJ.

Never changes unless you add or remove matter.

WEIGHT · N · peso

The force of gravity on that mass. Fw = m·g — depends on local g.

Your bathroom scale measures force, not stuff.

"You'd weigh less on the Moon." Did your mass change? What did?

WORKED EXAMPLE — TOGETHER FIRST

You, on the Moon

A 70 kg student stands on the Moon (g = 1.62 m/s²). What do they weigh?

GIVEN  m = 70 kg · gMoon = 1.62 m/s² ASKED  Fw = ? EQUATION  Fw = m·g
Fw = 70 × 1.62 Fw ≈ 113 N about 1/6 of your Earth weight (686 N) — and your mass is exactly the same 70 kg.
