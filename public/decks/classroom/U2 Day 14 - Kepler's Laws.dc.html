LAW 1 — ORBITS ARE ELLIPSES

The Sun Isn't
at the Center

Every orbit is an ellipse with the central body at one focus — off-center. That's why every orbit has a close side and a far side.

Earth: e ≈ 0.017 (nearly circular). A comet: e ≈ 0.9. 2026-XJ: e = 0.31 — noticeably squashed.

LAW 3 — WORKED EXAMPLE

How Long Is
a Martian Year?

Mars: a = 1.52 AU. Sun-orbit, so T² = a³ with T in years, a in AU.

GIVEN  a = 1.52 AU · Sun-orbit ASKED  T = ? EQUATION  T = √(a³)
T² = (1.52)³ = 3.51 T ≈ 1.87 years real value: 1.88. Kepler found this rule with no calculus and no telescope data of his own — just patterns.
