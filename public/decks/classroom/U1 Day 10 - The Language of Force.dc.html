The Force Family

MEASURED IN NEWTONS · 1 N ≈ ONE SMALL APPLE
GRAVITY

Pulls toward the center of any large body. Always on.

NORMAL

The surface pushing back. Perpendicular, always.

FRICTION

Resists sliding between surfaces. Points against motion.

APPLIED

Whatever pushes: a hand, an engine, a spacecraft.

TENSION

The pull along a rope or string. Only pulls — never pushes.
