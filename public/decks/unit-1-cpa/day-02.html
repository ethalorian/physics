CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 25 / 14
I do · count the travel

Distance counts every part

From 0 to 4 m: travel 4 m.

From 4 back to 1 m: travel 3 m.

Distance = 4 + 3 = 7 m
CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 26 / 14
I do · compare endpoints

Displacement compares endpoints

Start: 0 m. Finish: 1 m.

Change = 1 − 0 = +1 m

The cart ends 1 m right of where it began.
