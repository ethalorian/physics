Name the interaction partner.

Which object receives the forward force?

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 147 / 15
Solution and reasoning

A foot pushes the ground backward

The ground pushes the foot forward.

The foot receives that force; the ground receives the backward force.

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 148 / 15
Try it together

Are weight and support a third-law pair?

Earth pulls a book down. The table pushes the book up.

Are these two forces an interaction pair?

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 149 / 15
Solution and reasoning

Are weight and support a third-law pair?

No. They both act on the book.

The partner of Earth pulling the book is the book pulling Earth.
