The sign gives the direction

Right is positive.

+2 m/s means 2 m each second right.

−2 m/s means 2 m each second left.

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 39 / 15
Solution and reasoning

Does a high line mean fast?

No. Its position does not change.

Velocity = 0 m/s. A high position does not mean a high speed.
