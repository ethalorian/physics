Solution and reasoning

Repair: “The distance is zero”

Distance is 6 m, because the cart traveled 3 + 3.

Displacement is zero because the endpoints match.

Try it together

Repair: “Use the pull in F = ma”

A 2 kg cart has an 8 N pull and 2 N opposing friction.

A student calculates 8 ÷ 2. What step is missing?

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 197 / 16
Solution and reasoning

Repair: “Use the pull in F = ma”

Find net force first.

8 − 2 = 6 N
6 ÷ 2 = 3 m/s²

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 199 / 16
Solution and reasoning

Repair: “Negative means slowing”

Speed increases from 2 to 6 m/s, so it speeds up.

The minus sign tells direction.
