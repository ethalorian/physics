I do · find the change

One step at a time

Velocity changes from 2 to 6 m/s in 2 s.

Change: 6 − 2 = 4 m/s
CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 75 / 14
I do · find the rate

Share the change across time

4 m/s ÷ 2 s = 2 m/s²

Say it: “velocity gains 2 m/s each second.”

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 76 / 14
Try it together

Braking is acceleration too

A cart moves right at 6 m/s, then right at 2 m/s.

Did its velocity change? Is it speeding up or slowing down?

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 77 / 14
Solution and reasoning

Braking is acceleration too

It slows down. Its velocity changed, so it accelerated.

Here the acceleration points left, opposite the motion.

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 78 / 14
See another kind of change

Direction matters

A car turning at a steady speed still changes velocity.

The direction changes, even if the speed does not.

Solution and reasoning

Your turn: one calculation

8 − 0 = 8 m/s

CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 711 / 14
Check a common mix-up

Negative does not mean slow

Negative velocity means motion in the chosen negative direction.

Speeding up or slowing down depends on whether velocity and acceleration point the same way.
