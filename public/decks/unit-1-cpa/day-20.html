CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 204 / 15
I do · forces → acceleration

Step 1: find acceleration

Mass: 2 kg. Pull: 6 N right. Friction: 2 N left.

Net: 6 − 2 = 4 N right
Acceleration: 4 ÷ 2 = 2 m/s²
CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 205 / 15
I do · acceleration → velocity

Step 2: predict final velocity

The cart starts from rest. The forces act for 2 s.

Gain: 2 × 2 = 4 m/s
Final velocity: 0 + 4 = 4 m/s
CPA Physics / Unit 1 / Day 206 / 15
I do · velocity → displacement

Step 3: predict displacement

Average the start and final velocities because acceleration is constant.

Average: (0 + 4) ÷ 2 = 2 m/s
Displacement: 2 × 2 = +4 m

Step 4: include the starting point

The cart starts at 1 m.

Final position: 1 + 4 = 5 m
