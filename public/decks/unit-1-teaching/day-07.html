Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 76 / 21
Explanation and application

Acceleration measures velocity change

ā = (vf − vi) / Δt

Velocity can change in magnitude, direction, or both.

Speeding up, slowing down, and turning can all involve acceleration.

Braking in the positive direction

A car travels at +30 m/s and stops in 5 s. Find its average acceleration.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 79 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Braking in the positive direction

a = (0 − 30)/5 = −6 m/s²

The acceleration points opposite the positive velocity, so the car slows down.

Braking in the negative direction

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 711 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Braking in the negative direction

a = [−4 − (−12)]/2 = +4 m/s².

Speed falls from 12 to 4 m/s. The positive acceleration slows the negative motion.

Explanation and application

Constant acceleration is an assumption

v = v₀ + at
x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at²

These equations apply over an interval with constant acceleration.

If acceleration varies significantly, one constant value may not describe the whole interval.
