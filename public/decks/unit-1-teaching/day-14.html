Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 145 / 21
Explanation and application

One interaction, two force statements

FA on B = −FB on A

Both forces exist simultaneously. They have the same type and equal magnitudes, and act on different objects.

Naming the agent and receiver is the most reliable test for a pair.

Solution and reasoning

A hand and a wall

The hand’s diagram includes wall on hand. The wall’s diagram includes hand on wall.

Equal forces, unequal accelerations

During an ideal interaction, cart A has mass 1 kg and cart B has mass 4 kg. Each experiences a 12 N interaction force, in opposite directions. Ignore other horizontal forces. Find their acceleration magnitudes.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1411 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Equal forces, unequal accelerations

aA = 12/1 = 12 m/s². aB = 12/4 = 3 m/s².

Equal force magnitudes do not imply equal acceleration magnitudes.

Explanation and application

Balanced forces or a third-law pair?

A book rests on a table. Its weight and the table’s normal force are equal and opposite. Are they a third-law pair? Identify each force’s actual partner.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1413 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Balanced forces or a third-law pair?

No. Both act on the book.

Earth on book pairs with book on Earth. Table on book pairs with book on table.

The horse and cart question

The horse pulls the cart forward and the cart pulls the horse backward equally. How can the pair accelerate forward?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1415 / 21
Solution and reasoning

The horse and cart question

For the horse-plus-cart system, those internal forces cancel in the total. The ground’s external forward force on the horse can exceed external resistance, producing a forward net force.

The asteroid connection

A small spacecraft strikes a much larger asteroid. Compare the contact force magnitudes and the acceleration magnitudes during the interaction.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1417 / 21
Solution and reasoning

The asteroid connection

Contact force magnitudes are equal at each instant. The less massive spacecraft has the greater acceleration magnitude from that interaction.

A spacecraft does not need to survive intact to transfer momentum. Detailed collision analysis comes later.
