Explanation and application

The model and the bounds

  • Assume constant velocity for the entire interval as a deliberate teaching simplification.
  • Propagate the speed bound at the fixed 15° direction.
  • Let the initial x bound vary independently of speed.
  • Treat the angle, y₀, and elapsed time as exact for these bounds.
  • Discuss gravitational changes and Earth’s motion as excluded model effects.
