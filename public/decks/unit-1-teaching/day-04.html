Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 410 / 21
Explanation and application

A velocity vector has two components

A velocity of +4 m/s east and +3 m/s north is one diagonal motion with both components occurring at once.

|v| = √(4² + 3²) = 5 m/s
Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 411 / 21
Explanation and application

The angle needs a reference axis

Let θ be measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis.

vx = v cos θ
vy = v sin θ

Adjacent component uses cosine. Opposite component uses sine. Use degree mode for an angle in degrees.

Explanation and application

Guided components

A drone moves at 10 m/s, 30° north of east. Let east be +x and north be +y. Find both components and say which is larger.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 414 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Guided components

vₓ = 10 cos 30° ≈ +8.66 m/s. vᵧ = 10 sin 30° = +5.00 m/s.

The vector lies closer to east, so the east component should be larger.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 415 / 21
Explanation and application

Independent components

A second drone moves at 12 m/s, 60° north of east. Find the components. Explain why you do not add them to find the speed.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 416 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Independent components

vₓ = +6.00 m/s, vᵧ ≈ +10.39 m/s.

Perpendicular components form a right triangle. √(6² + 10.39²) ≈ 12 m/s. Their ordinary sum is not the vector’s magnitude.
