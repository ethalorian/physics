Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 105 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Forces on a seated student

Earth exerts weight downward. The chair and floor contacts provide support forces upward. Their vertical total balances weight in this stationary case.

Explanation and application

A force describes an interaction

Use the naming pattern force by agent on object.

“Rope on cart” and “Earth on cart” identify physical interactions.

Motion, speed, acceleration, and inertia are not additional force types.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 108 / 21
Explanation and application

Mass and weight

Fg = mg

Mass measures inertia in kilograms. Weight is a gravitational force in newtons.

Near Earth’s surface, use g = 9.8 m/s² unless a problem supplies another value.

A 5 kg object has weight ≈ 49 N

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1010 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Book on a table

Earth on book: weight downward. Table on book: normal upward.

The book’s force on the table acts on the table, so it does not belong in the book’s force list.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1012 / 21
Solution and reasoning

A hanging object

Weight = (2)(9.8) = 19.6 N down. With zero acceleration, tension is 19.6 N up.

A moving object after release

A ball has left a person’s hand and travels upward. Ignore air resistance. Which forces act after release?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1014 / 21
Solution and reasoning

A moving object after release

Only Earth’s gravitational force acts in this model. The hand no longer contacts the ball. The ball can move upward while its acceleration points downward.

Explanation and application

A surface tilted at an angle

A box rests on a rough ramp. Name the directions of weight, normal force, and the static friction that prevents downward slipping.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1016 / 21
Solution and reasoning

A surface tilted at an angle

Weight points vertically downward. Normal points perpendicular away from the ramp. Static friction points up the ramp in this case.

Independent force inventory

  • A box slides right while a rope pulls right across a rough horizontal floor.
  • A car coasts down a hill with air resistance.
  • An asteroid moves through deep space.
Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1018 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Independent force inventory

Box: weight, normal, rightward tension, leftward kinetic friction.

Car: weight, road normal, drag and any road contact forces required by the model. Asteroid: gravitational forces from external bodies.
