Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 210 / 21
Explanation and application

Guided route

Start at 2 m, walk to 6 m, then finish at 3 m.

Sketch the route. Calculate both distance and displacement.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 211 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Guided route

Distance = |6 − 2| + |3 − 6| = 4 + 3 = 7 m.

Displacement = 3 − 2 = +1 m. Position 3 m and displacement +1 m are different quantities.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 212 / 21
Explanation and application

Scalar and vector quantities

A scalar has magnitude and units. Distance 7 m and elapsed time 5 s are examples.

A vector also has direction. Displacement +1 m means 1 m in the chosen positive direction.

In two dimensions, an arrow or a magnitude with an angle can specify the direction.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 213 / 21
Explanation and application

Changing the positive direction

A person moves 3 m east. First choose east as positive, then choose west as positive.

What changes in the description? What stays the same physically?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 214 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Changing the positive direction

The displacement is +3 m with east positive and −3 m with west positive. The actual motion and 3 m distance do not change.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 217 / 21
Explanation and application

Why direction matters in the asteroid story

Two objects each travel 1,000 km. One moves toward Earth and one moves across our line of sight. Can distance alone tell us their future positions?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 218 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Why direction matters in the asteroid story

No. Equal distance can lead to different endpoints. We need the starting position and a displacement vector with direction to predict each endpoint.
