Explanation and application

Peer defense: two equal turns

First 7 minutes: A explains for 4 minutes. B asks questions and records evidence for 3 minutes.

Next 7 minutes: switch roles.

Listen for the reasoning behind the method. Record one specific strength and one question that could improve the work.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 228 / 15
Explanation and application

Questions that probe reasoning

  • What does your chosen origin represent?
  • How did you decide the component signs?
  • What quantity does this number represent?
  • Which uncertainties are included in your range?
  • What could make the real motion differ from this model?
