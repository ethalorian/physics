Explanation and application

Newton’s first law

In an inertial reference frame, an object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion keeps the same velocity, unless a nonzero net external force changes that velocity.

ΣF = 0 implies a = 0
Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 97 / 19
Explanation and application

Inertia and mass

Inertia is resistance to a change in velocity. Mass measures translational inertia.

With the same net force, a larger mass changes velocity more slowly.

Inertia is a property of the object, not an extra force arrow.

The passenger explanation

A bus brakes suddenly. A passenger’s body moves forward relative to the bus. Explain without adding a mysterious forward force.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 912 / 19
Solution and reasoning

The passenger explanation

The passenger initially shares the bus’s forward velocity. As the bus slows, the body tends to continue moving. A seat belt, seat, or other contact supplies a force that changes the passenger’s velocity.

Explanation and application

Evidence of a net force

  • A cart at rest remains at rest.
  • A cart moves straight at steady speed.
  • A cart turns at steady speed.
  • A cart slows while moving straight.
Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 914 / 19
Solution and reasoning

Evidence of a net force

The first two can have zero net force. Turning and slowing require a velocity change, so the net force is nonzero.

The asteroid connection

In an ideal region with zero net force, would an asteroid need an engine to keep moving? What must change for a deflection?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 916 / 19
Solution and reasoning

The asteroid connection

It would keep constant velocity without an engine. A deflection requires a change in velocity, produced by a nonzero net external force.

Real asteroids experience gravity, so their paths generally curve.
