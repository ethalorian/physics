Explanation and application

What does F mean?

A 5 kg box is pulled with 20 N right while friction acts with 5 N left. Is a = 20/5 the correct acceleration?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 135 / 24
Solution and reasoning

What does F mean?

No. The net force is 20 − 5 = 15 N right. a = 15/5 = 3 m/s² right.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 136 / 24
Explanation and application

A signed force sum

ΣFₓ = Fright − Fleft = maₓ

With right positive, a negative result means acceleration left.

Forces in a perpendicular direction require a separate equation.
