Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 165 / 24
Solution and reasoning

From arrows to a result

ΣFₓ = 30 − 10 = 20 N right. aₓ = 20/5 = 4 m/s² right.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 166 / 24
Explanation and application

Each axis gets its own equation

ΣFₓ = maₓ
ΣFᵧ = maᵧ

For a box constrained to a horizontal surface with no vertical acceleration, ΣFᵧ = 0.

Normal force equals weight only if those are the only vertical forces.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 169 / 24
Explanation and application

Axes along and perpendicular to a ramp

For a ramp at angle θ above horizontal, choose +x down the ramp and +y outward from its surface.

Weight component down ramp = mg sin θ
Weight component into ramp = mg cos θ

Weight itself still points vertically downward.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1611 / 24
Worked example

Worked ramp with given friction

A 4 kg box slides down a 30° ramp. Kinetic friction is 5 N up the ramp. No other forces act.

mg sin 30° = 19.6 N
ΣFₓ = 19.6 − 5 = 14.6 N
aₓ = 14.6 / 4 = 3.65 m/s²
