Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 612 / 21
Explanation and application

Decomposition from a stated angle

A 20 N pull points 30° above the positive x-axis.

Fx = 20 cos 30° ≈ 17.3 N
Fy = 20 sin 30° = 10.0 N

The components together represent the original force. They are not two extra forces.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 614 / 21
Solution and reasoning

The same angle measured from another axis

The angle is next to the y component. Fᵧ = 20 cos 30° ≈ 17.3 N.

Fₓ = 20 sin 30° = 10.0 N.

Reconstructing the asteroid velocity

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 618 / 21
Solution and reasoning

Reconstructing the asteroid velocity

Magnitude ≈ √(8,210² + 2,200²) ≈ 8,500 m/s.

θ = tan⁻¹(2,200/8,210) ≈ 15°. Small differences come from rounded components.
