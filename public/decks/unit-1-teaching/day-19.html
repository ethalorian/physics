Diagnostic A: motion graphs

A straight x–t line connects (0 s, 7 m) and (3 s, 1 m). Find velocity. Is 1 m the displacement?

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 196 / 24
Solution and reasoning

Diagnostic A: motion graphs

v = (1 − 7)/3 = −2 m/s. Displacement = −6 m. The 1 m value is final position.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 199 / 24
Diagnostic

Diagnostic C: forces

A 4 kg object has forces 18 N right and 6 N left. Find a. Name the third-law partner of Earth’s pull on the object.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1910 / 24
Solution and reasoning

Diagnostic C: forces

a = (18 − 6)/4 = 3 m/s² right.

The partner is the object’s gravitational pull on Earth.

Diagnostic D: prediction and bounds

Explanation and application

Station A: slope and motion

Repair: Label the axes. Circle two points. Subtract coordinates in matching order, then divide Δx by Δt.

Example: (1 s, 2 m) to (4 s, 8 m) gives (8−2)/(4−1) = +2 m/s.

Try: (2 s, 9 m) to (6 s, 1 m). Explain what a horizontal x–t segment means.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1914 / 24
Explanation and application

Station B: vector components

Repair: Draw the angle from its stated axis. Mark adjacent and opposite components. Decide signs from the quadrant.

Example: 10 N at 30° above +x gives (8.66, 5.00) N.

Try: 12 N at 60° south of east. Check the component sizes against your sketch.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1915 / 24
Explanation and application

Station C: force sum and object boundary

Repair: Name one object, list external agents, draw forces, then sum along one axis.

Example: A 2 kg object with 10 N right and 4 N left has a = (10−4)/2 = 3 m/s² right.

Try: A 3 kg object has 4 N right and 10 N left. Describe a and what extra information determines its motion direction.

Honors Physics / Unit 1 / Day 1916 / 24
Explanation and application

Station D: position and uncertainty

Repair: Convert units. Find central x₀+vt. Then calculate δx₀+tδv. State lower and upper bounds.

Example: 10±1 m plus (2±0.1 m/s)(5 s) gives 20±1.5 m.

Try: 20±2 m plus (−1±0.2 m/s)(5 s).
