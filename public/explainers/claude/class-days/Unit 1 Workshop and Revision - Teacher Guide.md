## Targets shown on screen
K1, K2 · R1–R11 · S1–S5, with short names taken from Days 1–18. **R3 (Day 5, predicting a position) is assumed:** check the codes and wording against your Unit Targets document and Self-Assessment sheet. Edit the list in workshop-unit1.jsx (the TG array) if anything differs.

## Using each stop
**STOP 1 · Self-assess (0–10 min).** Hand out the Self-Assessment sheet. The example sheet on screen shows what an honest one looks like: mostly 2–4 with a couple of clear weak spots. Push students to check ratings against real work (quizzes, exit tickets), not feelings.

**STOP 2 · Workshop (10–35 min).** Leave paused on the five stations. "Replay a lesson" can use any of the Day 1–18 in-class videos: open the file, press N to jump to the section they need, and B to replay it. Run coaching sign-ups in 5-minute slots.

**STOP 3 · Share (35–48 min).** One thing each, quick. The bubbles on screen are examples to prime the pump.

**STOP 4 · Exit ticket.** Look for a named target and a concrete plan (what, when, with whom). Vague plans ("study more") need a 1:1 follow-up before the transfer task.

## Formative checks
- **Self-assessment:** overconfident? Compare with your read of their work.
- **Workshop choice:** meaningful work or socializing? Walk the room.
- **Exit ticket:** concrete plan, or vague?
