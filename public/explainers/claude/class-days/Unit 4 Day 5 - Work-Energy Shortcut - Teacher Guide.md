## Answers and what to listen for

**STOP 1 · Quick check.** W_net = ΔK = K_f − K_i. Cart: (10)(5) = 50 J = ½(1)v² → **v = 10 m/s**.

**STOP 2 · Braking.** The braking force opposes the motion, so its work is negative: −(9000)d = 0 − ½(1500)(30²) = −675,000 J → **d = 75 m**. Watch the sign: both sides are negative.

**STOP 3 · Coaster.** Starts at rest, friction ignored. W(gravity) = mgh = (100)(9.8)(10) = 9,800 J = ½(100)v² → v² = 196 → **v = 14 m/s**. Point on screen: kinematics would need the track's shape (a curved track has changing acceleration); energy only needs the vertical drop.

**STOP 4 · Group practice (answers).**
1. 2 kg at 6 m/s, 4 N friction: K_i = 36 J; −4d = −36 → **d = 9 m**
2. 0.2 kg dropped 5 m: mgh = 9.8 J = ½(0.2)v² → v² = 98 → **v ≈ 9.9 m/s**
3. 1000 kg from rest, 2000 N net over 50 m: W = 1.0 × 10⁵ J → v² = 200 → **v ≈ 14.1 m/s**
4. 60 kg sledder at 2 m/s down an 8 m drop: K_i = 120 J; mgh = 4,704 J; K_f = 4,824 J → v² ≈ 160.8 → **v ≈ 12.7 m/s** (not √(2gh) alone: the starting KE counts)
5. 0.15 kg ball at 40 m/s caught over 0.20 m: K_i = 120 J; −F(0.20) = −120 → **F = 600 N**

**STOP 5 · Exit ticket.** 8 J = ½(0.5)v² → v² = 32 → **v ≈ 5.7 m/s**.
