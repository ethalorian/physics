## Demo numbers (example)
Elastic (magnet bumpers), right = +. Cart A 0.50 kg at +0.60 m/s hits cart B 0.50 kg at rest. Photogates read A +0.60 m/s before; after, B +0.57 m/s and A +0.02 m/s (A barely moves after the hit and keeps going right, away from gate 1, so it never passes back through it. Read A's after-speed from a motion detector or sensor cart, or time it later as it creeps through gate 2).

**STOP 2 · Your turn.**
- Σp before = (0.50)(+0.60) + (0.50)(0) = **+0.300 kg·m/s**
- Σp after = (0.50)(+0.02) + (0.50)(+0.57) = **+0.295 kg·m/s**
- % difference = |0.295 − 0.300| ÷ 0.300 × 100 ≈ **1.7%**. They match within experimental uncertainty; the small loss is consistent with track friction (a small external impulse).

Use your actual demo readings if they differ.

## Lab (STOP 3)
Two elastic trials (magnets) and two perfectly inelastic trials (Velcro). Record each cart's mass and **signed** velocity before and after. For Velcro, both carts share one final velocity v′. Compute Σp before, Σp after and % difference for each trial.
