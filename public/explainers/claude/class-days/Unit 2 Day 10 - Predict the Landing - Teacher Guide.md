## Answers (g = 9.81 m/s²)
**STOP 1 · Quick check** (Day 9's 20 m cliff at 15 m/s): t ≈ 2.02 s, x ≈ 30 m. Swap in your own Day 9 problem if it was different.

**STOP 2 · Stations** (answers appear on the next card):
| Station | Height | Speed | Time | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 m | 5 m/s | 1.01 s | 5.0 m |
| 2 | 20 m | 10 m/s | 2.02 s | 20.2 m |
| 3 | 50 m | 20 m/s | 3.19 s | 63.9 m |
| 4 | 100 m | 10 m/s | 4.52 s | 45.2 m |

Pattern: time depends only on height; distance = horizontal speed × that time. Station 4 is higher than 3 but lands closer because it's slower.

**STOP 3 · PhET.** Double the launch speed at the same height → **double the distance, same time**.

**Flyby preview (no stop).** Too slow: 2026-XJ strikes. Just right: it keeps falling around Earth. Fast: it falls a little and flies past. Leave the "just right" case for the orbit days.

**STOP 4 · Exit ticket.** t = √(2 × 45 / 9.81) ≈ **3.03 s**; x ≈ 15 × 3.03 ≈ **45 m** from the base of the cliff.

## Formative check
Order: vertical motion for the time, then horizontal motion for the distance, with **one shared time**. Watch for students who use x = ½gt² horizontally, or invent a separate "horizontal time."
