# Mic Check — 2026-09-28

Original call-and-response physics rhythm game at `/arcade/mic-check`; raw free practice at `/games/midway-mic-check.html`.

## Content and play

- Eight tracks follow the live course: Motion & Forces; Gravitation & Fields; Momentum & Collisions; Energy & Work; Thermal Physics & the Second Law; Waves, Sound & Light; Electricity & Magnetism; The Car Project.
- Six coach/response verses per track, 80 BPM, 90 seconds including count-in and an unscored freestyle finish. All units share the same 24-note chart and million-point cap so ranked timing scores use identical rules.
- D/F/J/K or pointer/touch pads; audible original synthetic character vocals and a procedural original beat. No microphone, student recording, external speech API, or copied commercial-game music/art.
- The student's existing equipped avatar is rendered with the shared sanitized Avatar component via a checked-in browser bundle. Unauthenticated/no-avatar players see a guest performer.
- Free practice offers every track. The ranked selector opens units through the furthest active unit in the student's physics course(s), using the existing course-pacing helper; staff can select all. This is a UI progression feature, not a security boundary or a mastery assessment.
- Paid Midway credit: 10 XP, including existing free-credit/staff rules. Rhythm scores never request learning-XP payout. Saving uses protocol 2, ownership validation, immutable final retries and explicit acknowledgement. Audio loads before a credit is requested; delayed credit replies cannot silently become a free run.
