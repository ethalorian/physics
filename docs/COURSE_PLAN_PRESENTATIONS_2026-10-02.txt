Course Plans presentations — October 2, 2026

Teacher flow
Open Teach, select Class → Unit → Course Plans day, and choose Present deck.
The default mode shares the deck and slide position between iPad and projector.
Course Plans “Present deck” and the day deck’s “iPad Teach” link open that day.
A CPA class selects its CPA deck; an Honors class selects its Honors deck.
A presentation keeps its selected edition when resumed, even if the class track changes later.
Days without authored decks remain visible but cannot be launched.

On the classroom computer, join the running presentation and open the projector
window. Keep the connection page open while controlling from the iPad.
Mirror this iPad remains an alternative display setup. “Present on this device
only” and rehearsal deliberately create no shared session.

Implementation
The session has exactly one source: lesson_id OR plan_unit_id + plan_day +
plan_track. Plan sessions have no lesson foreign key and do not depend on lesson
publication, student access windows, lesson numbering, or student block anchors.
The day catalog comes from the same authored plan data as Course Plans.
Existing explicit lesson sessions and their publication/ownership gates remain
compatible. Plan controls retain slides, blackout, timer, projector confirmation,
reconnection and private notes. Lesson activities are not shown for plan sessions.
The sidebar Teach link no longer carries a remembered student lesson. Resume
teaching includes the stored plan unit/day. The slide preview now scales within
its container at iPad and split-screen widths.
