# Pulse

Published and enabled on the live Midway: https://www.antocciphysics.com/arcade/pulse. Ranked runs cost 10 XP; practice remains free.

Pulse is a four-lane rhythm cabinet controlled with D/F/J/K or four pointer/touch pads. Its original synthesized track, **Neon Current**, runs for 75 seconds at 120 BPM, including a four-beat count-in. Six musical sections introduce bass, chords, and lead parts. The soundtrack and note judgement share the Web Audio clock, with output-latency compensation and a saved ±200 ms timing adjustment.

Practice offers Easy (72 notes), Arcade (174 notes), and Expert (274 notes). Hold heads and tails count separately. Ranked always selects the deterministic Arcade chart: 182 scoring events, maximum score 1,000,000. Misses never end the track. Extra taps break the streak and reduce the score. Practice bests are stored separately for each difficulty on the device.

Tab/window blur pauses playback. Resume includes a two-second countdown, during which held notes can be re-grabbed. Volume, timing offset, and reduced-motion preference persist locally. No external audio, font, or image downloads are required.

## Integration

- Entry: `public/games/midway-pulse.html`, with engine, music, controller, and CSS in `public/games/pulse/`.
- Migration: `supabase/migrations/20260928030421_add_pulse_cabinet.sql`. Registers `pulse` at 10 XP, `enabled=false`, under Midway. Reapplying it does not change an existing cabinet.
- Protocol 2: the shared bridge accepts `pulse-arcade`, acknowledges final scores, and permits retry. Pulse skips the learning-XP payout endpoint. The score route permits idempotent final retries after validating ownership.
- Preview: `public/arcade/previews/midway-pulse.webp`; mapped in the existing preview manifest. The capture script supports Pulse's attract screen.
- Deploy all assets and the two shared bridge/API changes before applying the migration and enabling Pulse through `/admin/arcade`.
