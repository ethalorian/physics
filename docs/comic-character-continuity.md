- Build: 5 feet 10 inches, about 240 pounds, an avid weight trainer. Render him as naturally strong and stocky, with moderate shoulders and substantial ordinary arms. Keep his torso, head-to-body ratio and height relative to the students consistent. No giant shoulders, exaggerated biceps, bodybuilder definition or shrinking/growing between panels.
- Hair: the ORIGINAL approved short, textured, slightly spikier cut. Modest natural lift and texture; approximately 55 percent silver-gray mixed with 45 percent dark charcoal across the top, front, crown and temples. Silver must remain visible throughout, not just at the temples. No flat side part, later blended variation, tall anime spikes, all-dark top or newly invented haircut.

Relaxed fit with natural ease at the chest and abdomen, ordinary shoulder width, sleeves around mid-upper arm, and a straight UNTUCKED hem just below the waist. The shirt drapes over his body; it is neither skin-tight nor oversized. Do not tuck it in, shorten it, stretch it over larger muscles, swap prints, or change sleeve length between shots.
- Pants: the SAME solid dark charcoal, nearly black trousers, comfortably straight fitted. Keep their base color dark in every panel. Warm reflected light may affect highlights but must not turn them khaki, tan, pale gray, navy or jeans. Keep the waistband under the untucked shirt.
- Shoes: simple black low-profile shoes, matching the approved classroom pages. No new bright soles, changed shoe colors or costume boots.
- Accessories: black smartwatch on his LEFT wrist. Do not invent new jewelry, glasses, hats, tattoos or accessories.
- Expressions and poses may change naturally; the face, hair, build, outfit and clothing fit remain consistent.
